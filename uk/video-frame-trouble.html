Що робити, якщо не працює

Can't pull images from a video. Here are troubles that commonly occur with Save Video Frames as Images and how to handle them.

Common troubles

  • "File is too large (up to 1GB)" appears: videos over 1GB can't be processed. Cut it shorter first.
  • "Too many images (up to 200)" appears: widen the interval.
  • "Could not process." appears: the video format may not be playable in the browser. Try another format.
  • The video won't play: the format or codec may be unsupported by the browser.
  • Saved images are blurry: the scene is mid-motion, or the original video is low quality.

Steps (when it doesn't go well)

  1. Check the video plays in the browser.
  2. Check the size is 1GB or less.
  3. For bulk, widen the interval.
  4. Try another browser too.
A video that can't play in the browser can't be made into images either. Convert to another format (such as MP4) and try.

Supported formats

MP4, MOV, WEBM and others. However, depending on browser support, some videos can't play. MP4 (H.264) is the most widely supported.

Saved the image but can't find it

  • Check the browser's download location
  • On a phone, check the Files or Photos app

Slow or stalling

  • Long or large videos take time
  • Close other tabs and apps to free memory
  • For bulk, use fewer images

Uses it suits

  • Pre-checks before trouble
  • Checking video formats

Приклад

Bulk saving showed "Too many images". Widening the interval from 2 to 5 seconds brought it within 200 and the ZIP was made.

Common stumbling blocks

  • Large videos on phones: can stall from lack of memory.
  • Black screen: a video may start with black. Change the playback position.

Frequently asked questions

Can audio be extracted from a video?

This tool targets pulling images. Use another tool for audio.

Підсумок

Review size, format and interval. That solves most troubles.
