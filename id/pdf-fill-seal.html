Cara Menambahkan Gambar Segel atau Stempel ke PDF

Diterbitkan: 19 Okt 2026 | Diperbarui: 19 Okt 2026 | Tentang (bahasa Jepang)

Poin penting
  • Cap di kertas putih lalu potret
  • Pilih "Gambar" lalu pilih fotonya
  • Ketuk halaman untuk menempatkannya

Japanese documents sometimes call for a seal (inkan) impression instead of a signature. Fill In a PDF: Add Text, Date, Signature and Marks has a feature for pasting a photo of a seal or signature onto a PDF. Here are how to use it and what to watch for.

Preparation: get a seal image

  1. Stamp the seal on white paper.
  2. In a bright place, photograph it from straight above with a phone.
  3. To reduce margin, crop around the seal with a crop tool, which makes it easier to use.

Langkah-langkah

  1. With "Choose a PDF", choose the document PDF.
  2. Under "What to add", choose "Image (seal and so on)".
  3. With "Choose image", choose the photo of the seal (the guide says "Choose an image (a photo of a seal or signature and so on)").
  4. Choose "White areas".
  • Make transparent: white areas of the image become transparent. Only the red of the seal sits on the document.
  • Keep as is: pastes it including the white areas.
  1. Tap the place on the page where you want it.
  2. Choose the size under "Size and color" and adjust the position with "Drag to move".
  3. Save with "Write and create PDF".
Choosing "Make transparent" removes the seal's white background, so it looks natural even when overlapping text.

Tips for a clean paste

  • Stamp the seal dark and clear
  • Avoid shadows when photographing
  • Use enough light that the paper's white is nearly pure white
  • Remove the background with "Make transparent"

Colour

The image's colours are used as they are. The colour in "Size and color" is a setting for text and signatures. A seal image is pasted in its photographed colour.

Contoh

Paste the seal image with "Make transparent" in the "seal" field of an application form.

Catatan

  • A seal image can easily be copied. Be careful about whom you share the PDF with.
  • Do not casually use images of important seals such as a registered seal or a bank seal.
  • Whether the recipient accepts a seal image depends on the document and the recipient. Check beforehand.

Common stumbling blocks

  • The seal's white background remains: set "White areas" to "Make transparent".
  • The seal is faint: stamp it darker and photograph it again.
  • Too big or too small: adjust under "Size and color".

Frequently asked questions

Can I use a seal stamped on a phone?

Use a photo of a seal stamped on paper. You cannot stamp onto a phone screen.

Is it an everyday seal or a registered seal?

A photo of any seal can be used, but handle important seals with care.

Ringkasan

Stamp the seal, take a photo and paste it with "Make transparent". Watch how you handle images of important seals, and the recipient's acceptance.
