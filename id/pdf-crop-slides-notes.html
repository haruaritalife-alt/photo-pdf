Memangkas Slide Kuliah agar Mudah Ditempel di Catatan dan Dipelajari Ulang

Diterbitkan: 19 Okt 2026 | Diperbarui: 19 Okt 2026 | Tentang (bahasa Jepang)

Poin penting
  • Pilih PDF slide
  • Pangkas margin secara otomatis
  • Gabungkan halaman dalam satu lembar lalu cetak

When you get lecture slides as a PDF, the margins are wide and they come out small when stuck in a notebook. Trimming the margins with Crop PDF Margins lets you place the slides larger on the same paper. It also makes them easier to read when you look back.

Why trim the margins

A slide PDF can have margin left around the screen. Trimming it means:

  • You can place the slides larger on the same paper
  • It is easier to stick into a notebook or write in the margin
  • The text looks larger when you review

Langkah-langkah

  1. With "Choose a PDF", choose the slides PDF.
  2. For "How to crop", if the slides are consistent page to page, choose "Use the same area for all pages".
  3. Set "Margin to leave around" to "A little (3 mm)".
  4. Press "Crop" and check the preview.
  5. Simpan.

Combine onto one sheet and print

Combining the trimmed PDF 2 or 4 per sheet with the "Combine PDF pages onto one sheet" tool also saves paper.

  • To write notes: 2 pages
  • To save paper: 4 pages

Since the slide margins are reduced, the text stays large even when combined.

If the slide edge has page numbers or a company name

Automatic trimming decides the margin including small text at the edge (page numbers, logos).

  • If the edge information is unneeded, use "Specify four sides in mm" and cut a bit more
  • If it is needed, leave "A little (3 mm)" and trim automatically

Steps (for sticking in a notebook)

  1. Make the PDF with trimmed margins.
  2. Combine it 2 or 4 per sheet.
  3. Print and stick it in the notebook.
Use class materials following your teacher's and school's rules.

Contoh

90 lecture slides: trim the margins and combine 4 per sheet to get 23 sheets. Stuck in a notebook, one class fits in 2 to 3 pages.

Common stumbling blocks

  • The crop is offset from slide to slide: use "Use the same area for all pages".
  • The text at the edge was cut off: increase the margin to leave.
  • There is a coloured background: automatic trimming may judge the background to be content and not trim it. Use "Specify four sides in mm".

Frequently asked questions

Does cropping change the slide content?

No. Only the visible area changes.

Does cropping alone save paper?

Cropping alone does not change the number of sheets. Combine it with the combine-onto-one-sheet tool.

Ringkasan

Trim margins to enlarge, then combine onto one sheet to save paper. Stick it in your notebook so it is easy to review.
