Cómo reducir una imagen a la mitad (por porcentaje)

Publicado: 19 oct 2026 | Actualizado: 19 oct 2026 | Acerca de (en japonés)

Puntos clave
  • Elegir imágenes
  • Elige «Por porcentaje»
  • Introduce 50 y guarda

You want to make an image half or a third of its size. You can specify it as a percentage without calculating pixels. Use "Specify by percentage (%)" in Resize Images by Pixel Size.

Pasos

  1. Choose the image with "Choose images".
  2. Under "How to set the size", choose "Specify by percentage (%)".
  3. Enter the percentage in "Value". 50 means half.
  4. Choose the "Format" (JPG, PNG or WebP).
  5. Press "Resize and save" and check the "Preview".
  6. Save with "Save image".
Width and height shrink by the same percentage. The ratio does not change.

Percentage guide

  • 100: as is
  • 75: three quarters
  • 50: half (half in both directions, so one quarter of the area)
  • 25: a quarter
  • 200: double (it gets coarse)

Ejemplo

A 4000×3000 px photo at 50% becomes 2000×1500 px. The file size drops to roughly a quarter.

Why it gets lighter

The pixel count falls in both width and height, so the area shrinks by the square of the percentage. At 50%, one quarter.

Adecuado para

  • Making an oversized photo smaller for now
  • When you do not want to think about pixels
  • Shrinking several images by the same percentage

When specifying in pixels

If the destination specifies something like "800 px wide", use "Specify width (px)".

Common stumbling blocks

  • A value above 100: the image gets bigger but coarse.
  • Too small: text becomes unreadable. About 25% or more is a guide.
  • You do not want to change the format: choose the same format as the original.

Frequently asked questions

Can I enter decimals?

Whole numbers are safest.

Resumen

For half, enter 50. Specifying by percentage saves calculating pixels.
