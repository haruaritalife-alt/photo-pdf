Cómo convertir medio ancho a ancho completo

Publicado: 19 oct 2026 | Actualizado: 19 oct 2026 | Acerca de (en japonés)

Puntos clave
  • Pega el texto
  • Elige de medio ancho a ancho completo
  • Copia el resultado

You want to make half-width letters and digits full-width: letters in names, or tidying the look of a document. Use "Half-width → full-width (letters, digits, symbols, spaces)" in Convert Full-Width, Half-Width, Hiragana and Katakana.

Pasos

  1. Paste the text in the "Enter or paste text" field.
  2. Under "Conversion type", choose "Half-width → full-width (letters, digits, symbols, spaces)".
  3. Check the "After".
  4. Copy with "Copy result".
To make half-width katakana full-width, choose "Half-width katakana → full-width katakana". That is a different conversion.

Examples of conversion

  • "ABC123" → "ＡＢＣ１２３"
  • "Tokyo 2026" → "Ｔｏｋｙｏ ２０２６" (the space also becomes full-width)
  • "(株)" → "（株）"

Adecuado para

  • Forms that ask for full-width input
  • Unifying half-width letters and digits in a document to full-width
  • Making digits full-width in vertical-writing documents
  • Full-width specifications for names and readings

Cautions when making things full-width

  • Letters, digits, symbols and spaces are targeted.
  • Email addresses and URLs stop working if made full-width. Do not make them full-width.
  • Phone numbers and postal codes are often required in half-width. Check the specification.

Full-width spaces

Half-width spaces become full-width spaces (wider blanks). The look of the document may change.

Digits in vertical writing

In vertical-writing documents, full-width digits can be easier to read. Follow the document's policy.

Ejemplo

The letters of a name "Taro Yamada" converted to "Ｔａｒｏ Ｙａｍａｄａ" to meet a form's specification.

Common stumbling blocks

  • An email address stopped working: because it was made full-width. Return it to half-width.
  • Symbols changed: "-" becomes "－" and so on.
  • Some parts are not converted: out-of-scope characters stay as they are.

Frequently asked questions

Can I revert full-width text to half-width?

Yes, with "Full-width → half-width".

Resumen

"Half-width → full-width" makes letters, digits, symbols and spaces full-width. Keep email addresses and similar in half-width.
