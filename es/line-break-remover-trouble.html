Qué hacer si quedan saltos de línea

Publicado: 19 oct 2026 | Actualizado: 19 oct 2026 | Acerca de (en japonés)

Puntos clave
  • Comprueba la opción
  • Vuelve a pegar
  • Mira el cuadro «Después»

You want to remove line breaks but they will not go, paragraphs vanish too, or words stick together. These are the problems that commonly occur with Remove and Tidy Line Breaks in Text and how to deal with them.

1. Line breaks will not go

  • Check that you chose a "Tidying method". If you choose nothing, nothing changes.
  • Choose "Remove all line breaks".
  • Check that the pasted text appears in "Before tidying".

2. Paragraphs vanished too

If "Remove all line breaks" is chosen, paragraph breaks go too. Choose "Keep paragraphs (blank lines)".

3. Paragraphs do not remain

"Keep paragraphs (blank lines)" works when there are blank lines between paragraphs. Text without blank lines is all joined.

4. English words stuck together

Choose "For English, put a space between lines".

5. Spaces doubled or too many

Also choose "Remove extra spaces".

6. You want one sentence per line but it does not happen

Choose "Break after sentence ends (。！？)". Check that the full stops are full-width. English "." is out of scope.

7. You cannot copy the result

Press "Copy result" and check that "Copied" is shown. If not, select the result text and copy manually.

8. The text disappeared

Input is not saved. Pressing "Clear" erases the input. Keep a copy before closing the page.

Order of checking

  1. Check the text is in the input field.
  2. Check the "Tidying method".
  3. Compare "Before tidying" and "After tidying".
  4. Try copying with "Copy result".
The input is never sent off your device. You can try as many times as you like.

Common stumbling blocks

  • Different kinds of line breaks: Windows, Mac and so on differ in line-break format. This tool normally tidies both.
  • The look does not change: the app you paste into may just be wrapping lines on display.
  • Words split by hyphens (English): not fixed automatically. Fix manually.
