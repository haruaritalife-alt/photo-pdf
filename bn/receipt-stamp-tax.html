You may have heard that receipts need a revenue stamp. What about electronic receipts? Here is how to think about it when making a PDF receipt with Create a Receipt PDF. This explanation is general. For actual treatment, check the National Tax Agency's guidance or the tax office.

What a revenue stamp is

It is a stamp-like label affixed to pay the tax (stamp duty) imposed on certain documents. A receipt above a certain amount is subject to it.

How electronic receipts are treated

In general, when a receipt is created as electronic data such as a PDF and handed over by email or similar, it is not treated as having created a paper document, and stamp duty is not charged. So a revenue stamp is not needed.

মনে রাখার বিষয়

  • When you print it and hand it over on paper: it is treated as a paper receipt. Above a certain amount, a revenue stamp may be needed.
  • Printing the PDF and giving it to the other party: if you hand over printed paper, it is treated as a paper receipt.
  • Handing it over as electronic data: a stamp is said to be unnecessary.

Steps (when handing over electronically)

  1. Make the receipt with this tool.
  2. Save it with "Save PDF".
  3. Send the PDF to the other party by email or chat.
If the other party wants a paper receipt, you print and hand it over. In that case, check by the amount whether a stamp is needed.

How to check whether a stamp is needed

  • Does the receipt's amount exceed the threshold?
  • Is it handed over on paper or electronically?
  • Does the transaction fall under the exempt ones?

These can change with laws and handling. Check the latest information in the National Tax Agency's guidance.

Check with the other party

Some business partners do not accept electronic receipts. Asking beforehand whether a PDF is fine gives peace of mind.

Common stumbling blocks

  • The other party asked for paper: print it and hand it over. Depending on the amount, a stamp may be needed.
  • You handed over paper without a stamp: it can be a problem for tax purposes. Check whether one was needed.
  • You put a seal frame on an electronic receipt: the frame is decoration and has no bearing on whether a stamp is needed.

Frequently asked questions

What if the other party prints the PDF I emailed?

Even if they print it, it is generally said not to create a stamp-duty problem for the side that made it. Check with the tax office for details.

Can I ignore it if the amount is small?

Amounts below the threshold may be exempt. Check the threshold.

সারসংক্ষেপ

Electronic receipts are said not to need a stamp. When handed over on paper, one may be needed depending on the amount, so check.
