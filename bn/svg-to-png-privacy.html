আমার SVG কি আপলোড হয়?

প্রকাশিত: 19 অক্টোবর, 2026 | হালনাগাদ: 19 অক্টোবর, 2026 | সম্পর্কে (জাপানি)

মূল বিষয়
  • যাচাই করুন
  • ব্যবহার করুন

Converting an SVG of a company logo or an unreleased design is a concern. Convert SVG to PNG converts an SVG to PNG inside the browser without sending it outside.

কীভাবে কাজ করে

  • The SVG you choose is loaded only inside your browser.
  • Conversion to PNG also happens on your device.
  • Neither the SVG nor the PNG made is sent to a server or stored.

ধাপ

  1. Choose the SVG with "Choose one SVG file".
  2. Choose the "Size" and "Background".
  3. Press "Make PNG and save" and save with "Save PNG".
Images and text information inside the SVG do not leave your device.

How to confirm

  • It can convert even with the connection cut (airplane mode).
  • Browser developer tools can confirm that no file is sent.

Still be careful about

  • Handling the PNG made: mind where it is kept and how widely it is shared.
  • Shared computers: when finished, delete the PNG and SVG.
  • SVG contents: an SVG holds information as text. Check the contents before handing it to others.
  • Cloud-synced folders: check that the save location is not uploaded automatically.

Information contained in an SVG

An SVG is a file written in text. Depending on the design software, the author's name or a work history may remain as notes. Before publishing, opening it in a text editor to check gives peace of mind.

Uses it suits

  • Converting unreleased logos and designs
  • Converting figures for in-house material
  • Converting personal works

উদাহরণ

Wanted to turn the logo of an unannounced new product into a PNG for slides. Did not want to upload to an outside service, but since it is processed in the browser, it could be made with peace of mind.

Common stumbling blocks

  • The preview disappeared when closing the page: by design nothing is saved. Save before closing.
  • Heavy with a large SVG: complex figures take time.

Frequently asked questions

Can the operator see the SVG?

No. Files are not sent outside.

সারসংক্ষেপ

SVGs are not sent outside. Mind where the finished files are kept.

“SVG থেকে PNG রূপান্তর” ব্যবহার করুন (বিনামূল্যে)

এর সঙ্গে ভালো মানায় এমন টুল

  • ছবির ফরম্যাট রূপান্তর (JPG, PNG, WebP): একসঙ্গে একাধিক ছবি JPG, PNG বা WebP-তে রূপান্তর করুন।
  • পিক্সেল মাপে ছবি রিসাইজ: প্রস্থ, উচ্চতা বা শতাংশ অনুযায়ী ছবি রিসাইজ করুন।
  • ফেভিকন (সাইট আইকন) তৈরি: একটি ছবি থেকে সম্পূর্ণ ফেভিকন সেট তৈরি করুন।
