Was der BMI nicht verrät

Veröffentlicht: 19.10.2026 | Aktualisiert: 19.10.2026 | Über uns (Japanisch)

Wichtige Punkte
  • BMI ermitteln
  • Als Richtwert betrachten

BMI is a handy index but not all-powerful. Here are the limits to know when looking at the result of BMI Calculator (with Reference Weight).

What BMI can't tell

  • Muscle-to-fat ratio: at the same BMI, someone with much muscle and someone with much fat are in different states.
  • Where fat is carried: it can't tell visceral from subcutaneous fat.
  • Blood pressure, blood sugar, cholesterol: it can't measure health itself.
  • Bone weight and body water: included in weight but not distinguished by BMI.

People it fits poorly

  • Athletes and people doing strength training: muscle weight makes BMI high.
  • Older adults: some views hold that slightly higher is better for health.
  • Children: in growth years, other criteria (growth curves, etc.) are used.
  • Pregnant women: weight gain can't be judged by BMI.

Steps (attitude when using the result)

  1. Enter "Height (cm)" and "Weight (kg)" and check BMI.
  2. Take the category as a guide.
  3. If concerned, also look at waist measurement, body fat percentage and checkup values.
Don't decide whether you're healthy from the BMI category alone.

Indicators to look at with BMI

  • Waist circumference
  • Body fat percentage
  • Blood pressure
  • Blood sugar and cholesterol levels
  • Daily condition and eating/exercise habits

Uses it suits

  • Knowing a rough guide to physique
  • Tracking long-term change on the same standard

Beispiel

A person who keeps up strength training got "Obese (class 1)" from BMI. Their body fat percentage was low and muscle mass high. An example BMI alone can't reveal.

Important

If you have health concerns, don't judge by numbers alone; consult a doctor, public health nurse or other specialist. This tool does not provide diagnosis.

Common stumbling blocks

  • Swinging with the numbers: daily weight fluctuation is normal.
  • Comparing with others: physique and constitution differ from person to person.

Frequently asked questions

If BMI is in the normal range, am I healthy?

Not necessarily. Lifestyle and test values matter too.

Zusammenfassung

BMI is a rough guide to physique. Judge health together with other indicators and specialists' opinions.
