iPhone-HEIC-Fotos in JPG umwandeln

Veröffentlicht: 19.10.2026 | Aktualisiert: 19.10.2026 | Über uns (Japanisch)

Wichtige Punkte
  • HEIC auswählen, JPG wählen, fertig
  • Mehrere Fotos auf einmal umwandeln
  • Umgewandelte Bilder funktionieren in Formularen und auf PCs

A photo taken on an iPhone may not open on a computer or upload to a form. The cause can be that the photo is saved in a format called HEIC. Convert HEIC to JPG converts HEIC (HEIF) images to JPG, which works anywhere. The conversion happens on your device and the photos are never sent outside.

Schritte

  1. With "Choose images", select HEIC photos. You can choose several at once.
  2. Check the list under "Selected images". Remove any you do not need with "Delete".
  3. Under "Output format", choose JPG or PNG. Usually JPG.
  4. Press "Convert".
  5. Save each with "Save", or all together with "Save all (ZIP)".
In some browsers, the first run takes a little time because a conversion component is loaded.

What HEIC is

HEIC is the standard photo format on iPhone. It looks about the same as JPG but the file is roughly half the size. However, some Windows computers and web services cannot open it.

Choosing JPG or PNG

  • JPG: suits most purposes. The file is light.
  • PNG: when you want to keep quality. For photos, it becomes quite large.

After conversion

  • The file name stays the same and only the extension changes.
  • The original HEIC photo remains as it is.
  • Some information, such as the shooting date, may be lost in conversion.

Common stumbling blocks

  • Nothing happens when you press Convert: the first run can take time to load. Wait a little.
  • A file cannot be read: it may not be HEIC or may be damaged.
  • Live Photos video: the video part is not converted. Only the still image becomes JPG.

Frequently asked questions

How many can I convert at once?

There is no fixed limit. A few dozen at a time is more stable.

Sinkt die Qualität?

JPG is slightly compressed when saved. In most cases you cannot tell the difference.
