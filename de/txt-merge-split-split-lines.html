Eine große Textdatei nach Zeilen teilen

Veröffentlicht: 19.10.2026 | Aktualisiert: 19.10.2026 | Über uns (Japanisch)

Wichtige Punkte
  • Datei auswählen
  • „Teilen“ und „Zeilenzahl“ wählen
  • Die ZIP-Datei speichern

A huge text with hundreds of thousands of lines: heavy to open in an editor, and can't be imported into systems. In such cases, split it by line count with "Split" in Merge and Split Text Files.

Schritte

  1. Choose the large file with "Choose text files".
  2. Under "What to do", choose "Split (by lines or size)".
  3. Under "How to split", choose "Lines" and enter the line count in "Per file".
  4. Check "Beginning of content" and press "Run and save".
  5. Save with "Save ZIP".
The split files are saved bundled in a ZIP. Open the ZIP and use them in order.

Deciding the line count

  • If there's an import limit, keep at or below it
  • If it should open in an editor, thousands to tens of thousands of lines
  • If unsure, around 10,000 lines

Beispiel

A 300,000-line log split into 10,000-line pieces makes 30 files. The last file holds the remaining lines.

Check before splitting

  • The total number of lines
  • The line count to divide at
  • Whether there's a header line (the header goes only in the first file)
  • File size (up to 50MB)

Using the pieces

  • Open the ZIP and use them in order
  • To find only a particular day's part, search the whole before splitting
  • To put it back, "Merge" restores it

Gut geeignet für

  • Investigating log files
  • Registering in systems with an import limit
  • Dividing the processing of large data

Hinweise

  • It doesn't split in the middle of a line
  • For files with extremely long lines, splitting by size is better than by lines

Beispiel

Split a 200,000-line access log into 10 files of 20,000 lines. It could be divided among staff to investigate.

Common stumbling blocks

  • The header is only at the top: the second and later files don't get headers.
  • Garbled text: only UTF-8 and Shift_JIS are supported.

Frequently asked questions

Can the split files be put back?

Yes. Connecting them in the order chosen with "Merge" restores it.
