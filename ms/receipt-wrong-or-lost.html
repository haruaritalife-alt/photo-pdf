Apa yang Perlu Dilakukan Apabila Resit Ada Kesilapan atau Hilang

Diterbitkan: 19 Okt 2026 | Dikemas kini: 19 Okt 2026 | Perihal (Jepun)

Perkara penting
  • Betulkan kesilapan dengan membuat resit baharu, bukan menyunting
  • Tandakan dengan jelas pengeluaran semula sebagai pengeluaran semula
  • Elakkan pengeluaran dua kali yang menyebabkan kekeliruan

You issued a receipt with the wrong addressee or amount, or you lost a receipt you received. Here is what to do. These are explained from both sides: that of the person making the receipt with Create a Receipt PDF, and that of the person who lost one.

Issuer's side: you issued it by mistake

  1. Tell the other party as soon as you notice: a receipt with the wrong amount or addressee cannot be used as it is.
  2. Make it again with the correct content: re-enter it in this tool and save a new receipt.
  3. Decide how to handle the old one: consult the other party and have them discard the wrong one, or treat it as "void".
Avoid a situation where there are two valid receipts for the same transaction. Tell the other party not to use the old one.

Issuer's side: you are asked to reissue

  • Check the content of your copy and make it with the same content.
  • Adding a note that it is a "reissue" helps prevent double expense claims.
  • Including a receipt number makes it easier to manage.

Recipient's side: you lost it

  1. Contact the issuer: ask for a reissue.
  2. Look for other evidence: bank transfer records, credit card statements, emails about the transaction.
  3. For expenses: consult your company's accounting. Even without a receipt, other documents may be accepted.

How to ask for a reissue

  • Tell them the date, amount and content of the transaction.
  • Confirm the addressee.
  • For an electronic receipt, having them resend the PDF is enough.

To prevent needing a reissue

  • Save it as soon as you receive it (a photo or scan will do).
  • Move electronic receipts into a folder so they are not buried in email.
  • Organize by date and business partner.

Common stumbling blocks

  • You cannot reach the other party: see whether other records can prove the transaction.
  • You got the addressee wrong: as with an amount mistake, make it again and decide how to handle the old one.
  • Correction tape or handwritten fixes: correcting a receipt invites trouble. Making a new one is safer.

Frequently asked questions

Can I fix it with a correction seal?

It depends on the case. For electronic receipts, making a new one is the norm.

Can I claim it as an expense without a receipt?

It cannot be said in general. Check with your company or a tax accountant.

Ringkasan

When you make a mistake, make it again and agree with the other party how to treat the old one. If you lose one, ask the issuer to reissue.
