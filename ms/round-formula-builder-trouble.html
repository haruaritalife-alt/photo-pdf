Apa yang Perlu Dilakukan Jika Tidak Berfungsi

Diterbitkan: 19 Okt 2026 | Dikemas kini: 19 Okt 2026 | Perihal (Jepun)

Perkara penting
  • Semak fail
  • Semak saiz
  • Cuba peranti lain

You built the formula but the result is odd. Here are common troubles with Build a Rounding Formula and fixes.

Errors on the tool side

  • "The input is not valid.": the cell to round isn't in the "A2" form, or digits/unit isn't a number.

Troubles after pasting the formula

  • #VALUE! appears: the cell to round is text.
  • #NUM! appears: MROUND with different signs for number and unit.
  • #DIV/0! appears: FLOOR with a unit of 0.
  • #NAME? appears: a typo in the function name.
  • Smaller (larger) than expected: digits and unit mixed up.
  • Totals don't match: look-only rounding mixed with function rounding.
  • Display still shows decimals: the cell format's digits hide the result.

Steps (when it doesn't go well)

  1. Check the cell to round holds a number.
  2. Check the function type and the meaning of its second argument.
  3. Compare the tool's sample result with the actual result.
If numbers display left-aligned, they may be text.

Displayed digits

A result showing 3.1 rather than 3.10 is a format matter. The value is 3.1; the trailing 0 just isn't shown.

  • Fix: align decimal places in the cell format

When totals don't match

If line amounts are rounded only by format, the total is computed from the originals and can differ by one from the visible sum.

  • Fix: round line items with ROUND, then total

Negative behavior

ROUNDUP and ROUNDDOWN go "away from / toward" zero. Take care not to confuse this with magnitude direction.

Contoh

Expected 200 from rounding up but got 100. The function was ROUNDDOWN. Checking it solved the problem.

Common stumbling blocks

  • Version differences: CEILING and FLOOR handle negatives differently by version.
  • Floating-point error: multiples of 0.1 can show tiny errors.

Frequently asked questions

ROUND's result differs from my calculator.

Calculators may hold more internal digits. Excel's ROUND rounds 5 up.

Ringkasan

Cell form, function type, digits vs unit. Check these three in order.

Guna “Bina formula pembundaran” (percuma)

Alat yang sepadan dengan ini

  • Kira Peratusan, Diskaun dan Perubahan %: Kira peratusan, diskaun dan perubahan % dengan serta-merta.
  • Kira Cukai Penggunaan (Termasuk / Tidak Termasuk Cukai): Kira harga termasuk dan tidak termasuk cukai.
  • Cipta formula SUMIF atau COUNTIF: Cipta formula SUMIF dan COUNTIF.
