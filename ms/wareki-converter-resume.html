Cara Menyeragamkan Gaya Tahun pada Resume

Diterbitkan: 19 Okt 2026 | Dikemas kini: 19 Okt 2026 | Perihal (Jepun)

Perkara penting
  • Pilih satu gaya
  • Tukar setiap tahun
  • Menyemak

On a résumé, years in your education and work history should be written consistently in either the Japanese era or Western calendar. Convert Between Western Years and Japanese Eras helps you convert Western years to the Japanese era.

Langkah

  1. Choose "Western year → Japanese era".
  2. Enter the years of enrolment, graduation, joining and leaving a company, one at a time.
  3. Copy the Japanese eras shown onto your résumé.
  4. You can also check them together in the "Quick reference".
Unify the résumé in either Japanese era or Western years. Do not mix them partway.

Basics of writing education and work history

  • Unify the years throughout (all Japanese era or all Western)
  • Write "Reiwa" and "Heisei" in full without abbreviating
  • Write "gannen" for the first year, rather than "1"
  • Keep months 1–12 consistent in half-width or full-width

Examples

  • April 2008, entered X Elementary School → Heisei 20, April
  • March 2014, graduated from X Junior High → Heisei 26, March
  • April 2019, entered X University → Heisei 31, April
  • March 2023, graduated from X University → Reiwa 5, March

Take care in years when the era changes

In 2019, January to April is Heisei 31, and May onward is Reiwa 1. Use them according to the month.

  • March 2019 → Heisei 31, March
  • May 2019 → Reiwa 1, May

Explained in a separate guide.

Check the destination's specification

Some companies specify Western years. Check the application guidelines or the specified format.

Checking with a list

The "Quick reference" shows how years and eras correspond. Handy when converting many years.

Common stumbling blocks

  • Japanese era and Western years got mixed: unify to one and rewrite.
  • Fiscal year versus calendar year: school years run from April to March of the next year. Check the year by the month of enrolment or graduation.
  • How to write "gannen": write "Reiwa gannen".

Frequently asked questions

Which is better on a résumé, Japanese era or Western?

Either is fine, but unifying matters. Follow any specification.

Ringkasan

Check year and month and convert to the Japanese era. Unify throughout, and mind the month in years when the era changes.
