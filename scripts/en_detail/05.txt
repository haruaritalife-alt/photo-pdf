@@ pdf-compare
## Before you compare PDFs
### When it helps
- You want to see how a PDF differs before and after edits
- You want to find the changes in a contract or document
- You want to know what changed in a new version you were sent
### How to read it
- Added lines are shown in green and deleted lines in red
- You can choose whether to show unchanged lines too
### What it cannot do
- Only text is compared. Differences in images or figures are not detected
- Scanned PDFs contain no text, so they cannot be compared
- For PDFs over 3,000 lines, narrow down the pages to compare

@@ pdf-crop
## Before you crop margins
### When it helps
- You want to trim the margins of a scanned PDF to make it easier to read
- You want text to look larger in a document read on a phone
- You want fewer margins when printing
### Choosing how to crop
- Trim margins automatically: trims the margin around text and figures page by page. Use this if unsure
- Use the same area on every page: makes the page sizes match
- Set all four sides in mm: cuts a fixed length from the top, bottom, left and right
### Things to know
The cropped part only becomes invisible and stays inside the file. It cannot be used to hide information you do not want seen. For that, use <a href="/pdf-redact.html">Black out parts of a PDF</a>. The file size does not get smaller. Text selection and search keep working.

@@ pdf-delete-pages
## Before you delete pages
### When it helps
- You want to remove blank or unneeded pages
- You want to take out pages that are not part of the submission
- You want to remove only the cover or some middle pages
### Tips for choosing
- Look at the thumbnails and tap the pages you want to delete
- You can also specify page numbers (example: 1,3-5)
- Find blank pages in the thumbnails and select them
### Things to know
- You cannot delete every page
- The change cannot be undone after saving, so keep your original PDF
- The text and figures on the remaining pages do not lose quality

@@ pdf-duplex-merge
## Before you merge double-sided scans
### When it helps
Use it when you scanned a double-sided document with a feeder that can only scan one side. It interleaves a PDF of only the fronts and a PDF of only the backs into one.
### Choosing the back-side order
- "Reverse": when you scanned all the fronts, then flipped the stack and scanned. The backs come out in reverse order
- "As is": when you flipped and scanned one sheet at a time
### Things to know
- If the front and back have different page counts, the extra pages go at the end
- After saving, check that the order is correct

@@ pdf-extract-images
## Before you extract images from a PDF
### When it helps
- You want to reuse only the photos in a document
- You want to take logos or figures inside a PDF out as images
- You want to use the original images in a PDF at their original quality
### How it works
The image data inside the PDF is extracted as is (saved as JPG or PNG). There is no loss of quality. When there are two or more, they are saved together in a ZIP.
### What cannot be extracted
- Things drawn as text or shapes (lines) are not images, so they cannot be extracted
- Images in some formats (such as CMYK) may not be extractable
- To turn whole pages into images, use <a href="/pdf-to-image.html">Convert PDF to images</a>

@@ pdf-extract-pages
## Before you extract pages
### When it helps
- You want only a few needed pages from a 100-page PDF
- You want to make a separate PDF with only the pages that must be submitted
- You want to email only part of a document
### How it works
Only the pages you choose become a new PDF, in their original order. Text and figures do not lose quality. To change the order, use <a href="/pdf-rotate.html">Rotate and reorder PDF pages</a>.
### Things to know
- The change cannot be undone after saving, so keep your original PDF
- Password-protected PDFs are not supported

@@ pdf-fill
## Before you write on a PDF
### When it helps
- You want to fill in an application or consent form without printing it
- You want to add a date or a tick to a document
- You want to stamp an image of a seal or signature onto a document
### What you can add
- Text, dates, ticks, circles and crosses
- Signature: draw with your finger (or mouse) inside the box. It is not saved or sent
- Image: choose a photo of a seal or signature stamped on white paper. "Make white parts transparent" removes the background
### Things to know
- Text you add is placed as an image. It cannot be selected or searched
- It cannot be undone after saving. Keep your original PDF
- If the place you submit to has its own electronic signature method, follow their instructions

@@ pdf-grayscale
## Before you make a PDF black and white
### When it helps
- You want to print a colour PDF in black and white
- You want to print while saving ink
- You were told to submit in black and white
### Choosing between the two types
- Grayscale (with shades): suits photos and figures
- Black and white (crisp text): suits documents with only text. Light-coloured text may turn white and disappear
### Good to know
- Pages are turned into images and made black and white, so text can no longer be selected or searched
- The file may get larger. If so, lower the resolution or use <a href="/compress-pdf.html">Compress a PDF</a>
- Password-protected PDFs are not supported

@@ pdf-header-footer
## Before you add headers and footers
### When it helps
- You want a document name or date on every page
- You want text such as "Confidential" on every page
- You want the document name next to the page number
### Placeholders you can use
- {n}: replaced by the page number
- {total}: replaced by the total number of pages
- {date}: replaced by today's date
### Things to know
- The text is overlaid on the page as an image. It cannot be selected or searched
- The change cannot be undone after saving, so keep your original PDF
- If you only need page numbers, <a href="/pdf-page-numbers.html">Add page numbers to a PDF</a> is simpler

@@ pdf-insert-blank
## Before you insert blank pages
### When it helps
- You want a blank page between chapters
- You want chapters not to start on the back of a sheet when printing double-sided
- You want to add blank pages to write notes on
### Ways to insert
- After chosen pages: enter page numbers (example: 2,5)
- After every page
- One sheet for double-sided printing: when the page count is odd, a blank page is added at the end so no back side is left over
### Good to know
- The blank page is the same size as the previous page
- Entering "0" puts a blank page at the start
- The change cannot be undone after saving, so keep your original PDF

@@ pdf-long-image
## Before you turn a PDF into one long image
### When it helps
- You want a single image that can be read by scrolling
- You want to show the content of a PDF on social media or chat
- You want an image of joined pages to see the whole thing
### Choosing settings
- Format: JPG if there are many photos, PNG if there is a lot of text or figures and you want it crisp
- You can also choose the width and the gap between pages
### Good to know
- If it would get too long, it is split automatically into several images and bundled in a ZIP. Phone browsers have a limit on the height of one image
- With many pages the image gets large and takes time
- Text can no longer be selected or searched

@@ pdf-metadata
## Before you remove PDF properties
### What are properties?
Information inside a PDF such as the author name, title, the software used and dates. They are not visible on the pages, but you can see them in the PDF's properties.
### When it helps
- You want to check that an author name is not included before sending a document
- You want to remove your company or personal name from the properties
- You want to rewrite the title
### Things to know
- What can be removed is the properties and, if you choose, annotations (comments and notes). Text and images written on the pages stay as they are
- If names or addresses are written inside the pages, use <a href="/pdf-redact.html">Black out parts of a PDF</a>
- A name can appear even if the author field is empty, because it may be in the software field or in text on the pages

@@ pdf-nup
## Before you put several pages on one sheet
### When it helps
- You want to use fewer sheets when printing a document
- You want to put 2 or 4 pages of a handout on one sheet
- You want a compact copy of a slide PDF for yourself
### How many pages per sheet
- 2 pages: gives a landscape sheet. Readable even for text-heavy documents
- 4 pages: suits slides and documents with little text
- 6, 8 or 9 pages: becomes quite small. Suits material that is mostly figures and photos
### When printing
The paper is A4. When printing, print at A4 and actual size (100%). If a scaling option is on, it gets even smaller. Even if the page sizes vary, they are shrunk to fit the frame and arranged.

@@ pdf-page-numbers
## Before you add page numbers
### When it helps
- You want page numbers on a report or document PDF
- You want continuous numbers across a PDF made from several documents
- You want the cover unnumbered and counting to start from page 2
### Choosing settings
- Position: bottom centre, bottom right, bottom left, top centre, top right
- Format: "1", "1 / 10" or "- 1 -"
- Set "Page 1" to "Do not add" to leave the cover unnumbered. Change "Number on page 1" to start from a later number
### Things to know
Only the number is added on top of the original page. Text and quality do not change. In a PDF with narrow margins, the number may overlap text. If so, change the position. If you also want a document name or date, you can use <a href="/pdf-header-footer.html">Headers and footers</a>.

@@ pdf-protect
## Before you add a password
### When it helps
- Before emailing an important document
- When storing a PDF that contains personal information
- When you want to hand it over with printing and copying prohibited
### How to share the password
It is safer to tell the other person the password by a different route from the PDF (such as a phone call or another message). If you forget the password, the PDF cannot be opened. For an important PDF, keep the original file.
### Good to know
- Encryption is AES-256
- You can also prohibit printing, copying text and editing. But these restrictions are rules that the software opening the PDF is expected to follow, and some software does not follow them
- The first time you use it, a processing program (about 1.3 MB) is loaded

@@ pdf-redact
## Before you black out a PDF
### When it helps
- You want to hide an address, bank account number or personal ID number before submitting
- You want to show only part of a contract or statement
- You want to remove personal information from a document you will publish
### Why it is hidden for certain
A blacked-out page becomes an image, so its text can no longer be selected or searched. The text under the black box is not left in the file either. Pages you did not black out stay as they were.
### Things to know
- Blacking out cannot be undone after saving. Keep your original PDF
- After saving, open it and check that the text under the black boxes cannot be selected
- To black out the same place on every page, use "Apply the same area to all pages"

@@ pdf-resize
## Before you match page sizes
### When it helps
- Scans or phone photos have left the pages in different sizes
- You want to match A4 for printing or submission
- You want to equalize sizes before merging several PDFs
### How it works
- Each page is enlarged or reduced to fit the paper while keeping its aspect ratio
- Leftover space is white. Nothing is cut off
- Text and figures are not turned into images and keep their quality
### Things to know
Small pages are enlarged. Images with low original resolution may look coarse. You can choose the size from A4, A3, A5, B5, B4, Letter or "Same as page 1".

@@ pdf-reverse
## Before you reverse page order
### When it helps
- The scanner read from the last page first
- You scanned each side separately and the order came out backwards
- You want to see the pages in reverse
### Types of ordering
- Reverse: the last page becomes page 1
- Swap every two pages: swaps the order of 1 and 2, 3 and 4 and so on
### Things to know
PDFs with many pages take a little time. Password-protected PDFs are not supported.

@@ pdf-rotate
## Before you rotate or reorder pages
### When it helps
- Some pages of a scanned PDF are sideways
- You want to change the order of pages
- You want to remove blank pages
### About quality
The page contents stay as they are, and only orientation and order change. So quality does not drop.
### Order of work
Fixing in the order rotate, reorder, delete avoids confusion. When you have finished, press "Create PDF with these changes" to save.
### Things to know
Password-protected PDFs cannot be used. Remove the password first.

@@ pdf-split-halves
## Before you split spreads
### When it helps
- You want a PDF of a book scanned as spreads to become one page at a time
- You want to split pages so they are easier to read on a phone
- You want to separate material with two pages side by side
### Choosing the binding
For scanned spreads, choose "Split left and right". For a right-bound book (vertical Japanese text), choose the "right to left" order, and the right page comes first.
### Things to know
It splits down the middle, so if the centre of the spread is off, it may not split cleanly. Each split page is half the size of the original page.
