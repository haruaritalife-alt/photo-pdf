@@ pdf-split
## Before you split a PDF
### When it helps
- You want one file per page from a combined PDF
- You want separate PDFs by chapter or document (specify ranges)
- You want to send it in halves to fit an email size limit
### Choosing how to split
- "One page each": makes every page a separate file
- "Every N pages": for example, a break every 5 pages
- "Specify ranges": split by document ranges such as "1-3, 4-6"
### Good to know
If you only need certain pages, <a href="/split-pdf.html">Extract or delete PDF pages</a>, where you choose while looking at the pages, is better suited. When it makes several files, they can be saved together as a ZIP. Password-protected PDFs cannot be used.

@@ pdf-to-image
## Before you turn a PDF into images
### When it helps
- You want to use one page of a PDF as an image on social media or in a document
- You want to submit a PDF to a form that accepts only images
- You want to keep the look of a PDF exactly as an image
### Choosing JPEG or PNG
- JPEG: smaller files. Suits documents and photos
- PNG: keeps text and lines crisp. Files are larger
### Good to know
- The limit is 150 pages. If you have more, split it first with <a href="/split-pdf.html">Extract or delete PDF pages</a>
- Once it is an image, text can no longer be selected or copied
- On iPhone, choose "Save" on each page, then "Save Image" to put it in the Photos app

@@ pdf-to-text
## Before you extract text from a PDF
### When it helps
- You want to copy and use the text of a PDF made from Word or similar
- You want to save the text of a PDF as .txt
- You want to search the content of a long PDF as text
### PDFs it can and cannot read
- Can: PDFs made from Word and similar, where the text is stored as text
- Cannot: PDFs that are only scanned paper. The contents are images, so text recognition (OCR) is needed
### Good to know
- For PDFs with tables or columns, the text order and line breaks may differ from the original
- Some PDFs may show garbled text, because the text data is stored in a special form
- Password-protected PDFs cannot be used

@@ pdf-to-word
## Before you turn a PDF into Word
### When it helps
- You want to rewrite the text of a PDF in Word
- You want the text of a PDF in an editable form
- You want to quote or summarize the text of a long PDF
### What it can and cannot do
- Can: take the text out of a PDF and put it in Word (.docx)
- Cannot: reproduce the layout of tables, images and columns. You get a text-only Word file
- Scanned PDFs have no text, so you first need <a href="/ocr-text.html">OCR</a>
### Good to know
To get the exact look in Word, you need dedicated software. This tool suits cases where you want to edit the text again.

@@ pdf-unlock
## Before you remove a PDF password
### When it helps
- You are tired of typing the password every time
- You want to use a PDF yourself that has printing or copying restrictions
- You want to unlock a PDF whose password you know, to merge or compress it
### PDFs you may use it on
Use it only on PDFs whose password you know and that you are allowed to use. There is no feature to guess or crack passwords. A PDF whose password you have forgotten cannot be opened.
### Good to know
- A PDF with only restrictions does not need a password
- The first time you use it, a processing program (about 1.3 MB) is loaded
- Neither the PDF nor the password is sent outside your device

@@ pdf-watermark
## Before you add a watermark
### When it helps
- You want to write "for submission" on a document so its purpose is clear
- You want a faint "Copy" or "Confidential" on handouts
- You want "Sample" on a draft PDF
### Choosing how to add it
- Placement: choose from centre, all over, or bottom right
- You can also choose direction, size, colour and opacity. Check the preview
- The text can be up to 30 characters
### Things to know
- The watermark is overlaid on the page. Text and figures may become slightly harder to see
- A watermark can be removed by someone determined. It is not a way to protect secrets
- A watermark you have added cannot be removed with this tool. Keep the original PDF

@@ percent-calculator
## Before you calculate percentages
### Calculations available
- B% of A: for example, 20% of 5,000 yen
- A is what % of B: for example, what % of 200 is 30
- B% off: the price after a discount
- % change: the rate of increase or decrease
### How it is calculated
20% off means multiplying the original price by 0.8, which is 100 minus the discount rate, divided by 100.
### Things to know
- For discounts, enter prices consistently either with tax or without
- When B is 0, the percentage cannot be calculated
- Decimals can be entered

@@ photo-to-pdf
## Before you turn photos into a PDF
### When it helps
- You want to submit documents photographed on your phone (applications, certificates, receipts) as one PDF
- You want to submit photos of handwritten notes or diagrams as an assignment
- You want to gather paper material into one file you can email
### Choosing settings
- Page size: choose "A4" or "Same size as the photo"
- Margin: choose "None" or "Small"
- Quality: when there is a size limit, use "Set a size limit" and choose 1, 2, 5 or 10 MB
### Tips for a clean result
- Shoot in a bright place, from directly above
- Avoid shadows and make sure all four corners of the document are in the picture
- Fix photos with the wrong orientation with "Rotate" before making the PDF
### Good to know
The photos are put into the PDF as they are, so the text in the PDF cannot be selected. If you cannot select iPhone HEIC photos, first use <a href="/heic-to-jpg.html">HEIC to JPG</a>.

@@ pptx-to-text
## Before you extract text from PowerPoint
### When it helps
- You want to use the text of slides for preparing or reviewing material
- You want to check a presentation script as text
- You want to search the content of slides as text
### How it extracts
- Text is extracted slide by slide

### What cannot be extracted

@@ qr-code-maker
## Before you make a QR code
### When it helps
- You want to put your website URL on a shop flyer or business card
- You want guests to scan a Wi-Fi name and password
- You want people to open an online document from paper material
### Tips for easy scanning
- Use a white background and a dark pattern. Light colours are hard to scan
- More text makes the pattern finer. A short URL is easier to scan
- When printing, do not trim off the white margin around it
### Check before use
Take care with typos in the URL. Open the URL to check before you make the code. After making it, scan it with a phone camera to check. The QR codes made here do not expire. But if the page it links to disappears, it can no longer be opened.

@@ random-picker
## Before you draw lots or decide an order
### Three functions
- Draw lots: picks a winner from names or items
- Order: decides an order for presentations or seats at random
- Group split: divides people into groups according to the number
### Tips
- Enter one name or item per line
- You can redo it any number of times. Each time it picks anew
- It uses the browser's random numbers (crypto) so the choice is not biased
### Things to know
If you enter the same name twice, it gets two chances. For an important draw, keep a record of the result.

@@ receipt-maker
## Before you make a receipt
### When it is fine to use
Issue a receipt only for money you actually received. A receipt that differs from the facts can break the law. It can be used for gatherings of individuals or a small shop's receipt copy.
### Items to enter
- Addressee (the person or company that paid): if left blank, "Sir/Madam" (上様) is entered
- Amount (tax included), tax breakdown, description, issue date
- Issuer (your name or organization): enter an address or registration number only when needed
### Good to know
- An A4 PDF is created. The text is placed as an image, so it cannot be copied
- Revenue stamps and the handling of invoices change depending on conditions. If unsure, ask the tax office or a tax accountant
- What you enter is never sent outside your device

@@ regex-tester
## Before you test a regular expression
### When it helps
- You want to check that a regular expression matches as you expect
- You want to find matching places in text
- You want to try the result of a replacement
### What the flags mean
- i: ignore case
- m: treat each line separately
- s: lets "." match line breaks too
### Good to know
- It uses the regular expressions of the browser's JavaScript
- It finds all matches even if you do not write a flag
- In replacements, $1 and $& can be used. Very complex patterns may run slowly

@@ resume-maker
## Before you make a résumé
### What you get
A two-page A4 PDF. It can be used both for email submission and for printing. The photo is placed in a 40 mm tall by 30 mm wide ratio.
### Things to watch when writing
- Write your career and qualifications truthfully. Content that differs from the facts can cause trouble later
- If text does not fit in a box, an error appears. Shorten the text
- First check whether the place you submit to specifies a fixed format
### About privacy
What you enter and your photo are never sent outside your device. The draft stays only inside the browser on this device. On a shared computer, do not use draft saving.
### What to do next
If the place you submit to has a PDF size limit, you can use <a href="/compress-pdf.html">Compress a PDF</a>. You can prepare the photo with <a href="/id-photo.html">ID photo</a>.

@@ round-formula-builder
## Before you build a rounding formula
### When it helps
- You want to round amounts or numbers to a set number of digits
- You want to align to units like 10 or 0.5
- You want to handle fractions of consumption tax
### Choosing a function
- ROUND: rounds off. ROUNDUP: rounds up. ROUNDDOWN: rounds down
- CEILING, FLOOR and MROUND: round to a unit such as 10 or 0.5
### How digits work
With 0 digits you get a whole number, and with 2 you get two decimal places. If you enter a test number, you can also check an example result.

@@ sheet-name-checker

### When it helps
Use it when naming a sheet gives an error. If there is a problem, the reason appears below.
### Sheet name rules
- Up to 31 characters
- Characters that cannot be used are replaced with _

Enter a sheet name and the corrected name appears. Copy it to use.

@@ split-bill-calculator
## Before you split a bill
### When it helps
- You want to split the cost of a party or trip
- You want to round each person's share up, for example to the nearest 100 yen
### How rounding up works
Each share is rounded up to the unit you set, so the total is equal to or more than the actual amount. The leftover can go toward the organizer's effort or the next fee.

- If you want different amounts for different people, calculate them separately

@@ split-pdf
## Before you extract or delete pages
### When it helps
- You want to pull out only the pages needed for submission
- You want to remove blank or unneeded pages
- You want to send only some pages of a contract or similar
### Tips
- Tap pages to tick them. You can also choose by range, such as "1-3, 5"
- Always check whether "Keep (delete the rest)" or "Delete" is chosen before saving
- The page order does not change. Only the pages you chose remain, in their original order
### Things to know
Small images of the pages are made, so with many pages it may take some time to show. If you only want to hide certain parts, rather than deleting pages, use <a href="/pdf-redact.html">Black out parts of a PDF</a>.

@@ sumif-formula-builder
## Before you build SUMIF and COUNTIF formulas
### When it helps
- You want to total only the cells that match a condition
- You want to count the cells that match a condition
- You want the average of the cells that match a condition
### How the formula is built
- You can choose from SUMIF, COUNTIF and AVERAGEIF
- Enter the range to test, the condition and the range to total
- The condition can be a comparison such as ">=80"
### For several conditions
Formulas such as SUMIFS, which work for several conditions, are built at the same time. COUNTIF does not use a range to total.

@@ table-paste-convert
## Before you convert a pasted table
### When it helps
- You want to paste an Excel table into a blog or document
- You want a table as Markdown, HTML, CSV or JSON
- You want to convert just by copy and paste, without opening a file
### How it works
A table copied in Excel is tab-separated text. The tool reads it and converts it. You can choose whether the first row is the heading.

@@ table-transpose
## Before you swap table rows and columns
### When it helps
- You want to turn vertical data horizontal
- You want to change the orientation of a table
- You want to skip Excel's "Transpose" feature
### How to use it
Copy a table and paste it, and the table with rows and columns swapped appears right below. The result is tab-separated, so you can paste it straight into Excel.
### Things to know
- When rows have different numbers of columns, the missing parts are filled with empty cells
- Line breaks inside cells are not handled by this tool
