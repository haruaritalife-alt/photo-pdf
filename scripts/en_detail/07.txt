@@ tax-calculator
## Before you calculate consumption tax
### When it helps
- You want to check the amounts on a receipt or invoice
- You want the tax-included price from a price without tax
- You want the tax amount from a tax-included amount
### How it is calculated
- Formula for the price without tax from a tax-included price: tax-included price divided by (1 + tax rate). At 10%, that is the tax-included price divided by 1.1
- Choose the rate from 10% and 8%. 8% is the reduced rate for food and drink and similar
### Things to know
Match the rounding of fractions (down, off or up) to the rules of the invoice or shop. The result of this tool is a guide to the calculation. For amounts on official documents, follow the instructions of your business partner or tax accountant.

### When it helps

- You want to change the form of letters in a heading
- You want English text in all capitals or all lowercase

- In forms such as camelCase, characters other than letters and digits are removed

@@ timestamp-converter
## Before you convert Unix time
### When it helps
- You want to read a number like 1767225600 in a log or API as a date and time
- You want to turn a date and time into a Unix time number
- You want to check the difference between UTC and Japan time
### How it works
Unix time is the number of seconds since 00:00 on January 1, 1970 (UTC). Large numbers of about 100 billion (11 digits) or more are read as milliseconds.
### Things to know
- The time zone shown follows your device's setting

- Your device's time zone, UTC and ISO 8601 times are shown side by side

@@ unit-converter
## Before you convert units
### Conversions available
- Length, weight, area, volume, data size and temperature
- Japanese units such as tsubo, tatami, shaku and kan are also supported
### Reference numbers
- 1 tsubo: about 3.3058 square metres, which is about 2 tatami

@@ url-encoder
## Before you encode a URL
### When it helps
- You want to turn a garbled-looking URL back into readable form
- You are putting search words and similar into a URL
- You want to put a URL containing Japanese into the correct form
### The difference between the two modes
- "Part of text": also converts ?, & and =. Use it when putting search words into a URL
- "Whole URL": leaves ? and &. Use it when you only want to fix the Japanese part of an existing URL
### Good to know
Characters not allowed in URLs (such as Japanese and spaces) are replaced with the form "%E3...". Decoding is the reverse. When decoding in "Part of text" mode, "+" is read as a space.

@@ uuid-generator

### When it helps

- You want to make many non-overlapping IDs at once
- You want to specify a format such as "uppercase" or "no hyphens"

A UUID is an identifier string made so as not to overlap (version 4). It is made with the browser's random number feature. The probability of getting the same UUID twice is negligibly small in practice.

- A UUID is no substitute for a password. For passwords, use <a href="/password-generator.html">Password generator</a>

@@ wareki-converter
## Before you convert Japanese era years
### When it helps
- You want to write the years of education and work history on a Japanese résumé in the era system
- You want to convert era years on official documents to Western years
- You want to write a date of birth in the era system
### Calculation guide
- Reiwa = Western year - 2018 (2026 is Reiwa 8)
- Heisei = Western year - 1988, Showa = Western year - 1925
- Taisho = Western year - 1911, Meiji = Western year - 1867
### Years when the era changed
In 2019, January to April is Heisei 31 and from May it is Reiwa 1. In 1989, 1926 and 1912 the era also changed partway through the year. When you know the month and day, decide by that date. Dates before 1868 cannot be converted.

@@ xlookup-formula-builder
## Before you build an XLOOKUP formula
### When it helps
- You want to look up values in a table more flexibly than with VLOOKUP
- You want a value from a column to the left of the lookup column
- You want to search from the bottom, or look for the nearest value
### Choosing a type
- XLOOKUP: returns the value in the return column for the row found in the lookup column
- XMATCH: returns the number of the position found
- LAST: searches from the bottom. APPROX: looks for the nearest smaller value. REVERSE: returns a value from a column on the left, using a column on the right
### For older Excel
The second formula also works in older Excel that does not have XLOOKUP.

@@ zenkaku-hankaku
## Before you convert full-width and half-width characters
### When it helps
- You want to match the full-width or half-width rule of an application form
- You want to make the letters and digits in copied text consistent
- You want to change hiragana to katakana, or the reverse
### Guide to which to use
- File names and email addresses: use half-width letters and digits
- Half-width katakana: it easily becomes garbled, so normally make it full-width

### Good to know
It converts full-width letters, digits and symbols to half-width and back. Full-width and half-width katakana, and hiragana and katakana, can also be converted. The text you enter is never sent outside your device.
