अगर रंग न पढ़ा जा सके तो क्या करें

You want to find a colour from an image but cannot, cannot copy, or the colour does not match. These are the problems that commonly occur with Pick Color Codes from an Image and how to deal with them.

1. The image cannot be processed

If "The image could not be processed. The format may not be supported." is shown:

  • For HEIC, first convert to JPG with "HEIC to JPG".
  • Check whether another app can open it.
  • Change to JPG or PNG and try again.

2. No colour appears when tapping

  • Check that the image is displayed.
  • Check that you are tapping (clicking) on the image.
  • Reload the screen and try again.

3. You press "Copy" but cannot paste

  • Check that "Copied" was shown.
  • In the app you paste into, do the paste operation (long press or Ctrl+V).
  • Check that copying is not restricted by the browser settings.

4. The colour differs from what you expected

  • Choose a part with uniform colour.
  • Zoom in on the image and tap the intended spot.
  • Also check the reasons colours look different, explained in a separate guide.

5. Main colours do not appear

  • The image may be too large, or processing is heavy. Wait a little.
  • Try another image.

6. You cannot tap the intended spot

Zoom the image before tapping. On a phone, widen the screen to make it easier to operate.

7. The colour code format does not fit

A colour code is in the form "#RRGGBB". If the app you use takes another format (such as RGB numbers), conversion is needed.

Order of checking

  1. Check the image format.
  2. Check that the image is displayed.
  3. Tap and check a colour code appears.
  4. Copy and try pasting.
The original image does not change. You can check as many times as you like.

Common stumbling blocks

  • Heavy on a phone: a large image makes processing heavy. You can shrink it first.
  • The colour differs each time: in photos colour differs by spot. Choose a uniform part.

“इमेज से रंग कोड चुनें” इस्तेमाल करें (मुफ़्त)

इसके साथ काम आने वाले टूल

  • इमेज क्रॉप करें और घुमाएँ: इमेज को किसी भी हिस्से या अनुपात में क्रॉप करें और घुमाएँ।
  • ब्लैक-एंड-व्हाइट, सेपिया और दूसरे लुक लगाएँ: तस्वीरों को ब्लैक-एंड-व्हाइट या सेपिया में बदलें, या चमक बदलें।
  • इमेज का आकार पिक्सल में बदलें: इमेज का आकार चौड़ाई, ऊँचाई या प्रतिशत के हिसाब से बदलें।
