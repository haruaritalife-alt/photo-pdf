ईमेल में कैलेंडर में जोड़ने का लिंक जोड़ना

प्रकाशित: 19 अक्तू॰ 2026 | अपडेट: 19 अक्तू॰ 2026 | परिचय (जापानी)

मुख्य बातें
  • इवेंट दर्ज करें
  • लिंक कॉपी करें

A link like "📅 Add to calendar" at the end of a notice email is a big help to recipients. Here is how to attach a link made with Google Calendar Add-Event Link Maker (with ICS) to an email.

चरण

  1. Enter "Event title", "Date", "Start time" and "End time".
  2. If needed, enter "Location (optional)" and "Notes (optional)".
  3. Press "Copy" for the "Add-to-calendar link".
  4. Paste the link into the email body.
A long link is hard to read if pasted as is, so setting a link on text with your email software's "Insert link" looks better.

Email text examples

  • "On the day, you can add the event to your calendar from the link below."
  • "▶ Add to calendar: (link)"

A short note before and after makes a considerate notice.

Setting it in email software

Most email software lets you set a link on text.

  1. Write "Add to calendar" and select that text.
  2. Choose "Insert link".
  3. Paste the copied link.

इनके लिए अच्छा

  • Meeting invitation emails
  • Seminar and event notice emails
  • Notices of interview and appointment schedules
  • Internal notices of regular meetings

Choosing between link and ICS file

  • Link: for people using Google Calendar. Just paste into the email.
  • ICS file: for people using other calendars such as Outlook or Apple Calendar. Attach the file.

When you don't know the recipient's calendar, preparing both is reliable.

उदाहरण

Added an add-to-calendar link to a seminar notice email for outsiders. Many participants used Google Calendar and could add it in one click, so no-shows on the day fell.

Common stumbling blocks

  • The long link breaks up: set it as a link rather than raw text in the email.
  • The email software disables links: security settings may prevent opening links.
  • Won't open on phones: without the Google Calendar app, it opens in the browser.

Frequently asked questions

Does opening the link overwrite the opener's existing events?

No. It becomes a screen for adding a new event; existing events don't change.

सारांश

Put an add-to-calendar link in notice emails. It reduces recipients' effort and forgetting.
