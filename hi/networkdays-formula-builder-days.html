दिनों का अंतर

प्रकाशित: 19 अक्तू॰ 2026 | अपडेट: 19 अक्तू॰ 2026 | परिचय (जापानी)

मुख्य बातें
  • DAYS चुनें
  • तारीख़ें भरें
  • जाँच लें

Sometimes you want the plain difference in days rather than business days, such as how many days passed since a contract. "DAYS" and "DAYS-INCL" in Build a NETWORKDAYS Formula build those formulas.

The two methods

  • DAYS: end − start. One of the two ends isn't counted.
  • DAYS-INCL: DAYS plus 1, counting both ends.

चरण

  1. Choose "DAYS" or "DAYS-INCL" in "Method".
  2. शुरुआती और आख़िरी तारीख़ के सेल डालें।
  3. Check the result with sample dates.
  4. Copy the formula and paste.
The DAYS function takes arguments as "end, start". The tool orders them for you.

उदाहरण

  • DAYS: =DAYS(B2,A2)
  • DAYS-INCL: =DAYS(B2,A2)+1

For 2026-10-01 to 2026-10-07, DAYS gives "6" and DAYS-INCL gives "7".

कौन-सा इस्तेमाल करें

  • Elapsed days (how many days have passed): DAYS
  • Length of a period (how many days long): DAYS-INCL

इनके लिए अच्छा

  • Days elapsed on a contract or loan
  • Length of an event in days
  • Length of a trip

Plain subtraction also works

=B2-A2 gives the same result. If it shows as a date, set the display format to "Number".

उदाहरण

A trip ran October 1 to 7 and I wanted the number of days. Since both ends count, DAYS-INCL gave 7 days.

Common stumbling blocks

  • End date earlier: the result is negative.
  • Older Excel: DAYS needs Excel 2013 or later.

Frequently asked questions

I want months or years

Use DATEDIF.

सारांश

For plain day differences use DAYS; add 1 to count both ends.
