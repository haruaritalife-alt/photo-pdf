Excel कॉलम का जोड़ ज्यों का त्यों निकालना

प्रकाशित: 19 अक्तू॰ 2026 | अपडेट: 19 अक्तू॰ 2026 | परिचय (जापानी)

मुख्य बातें
  • कॉलम कॉपी करें
  • पेस्ट करें
  • नतीजा देखें

You want to quickly summarise the numbers in an Excel column. With Sum, Average and Median of a Column of Numbers, just copy the column and paste.

चरण

  1. In Excel, select the number column (or range) and copy.
  2. Paste into "Numbers (one per line, or separated)".
  3. Check the six values.
A copied Excel column pastes as one number per line.

Correspondence with Excel functions

This tool's results match these Excel functions.

  • Count: COUNT
  • Sum: SUM
  • Average: AVERAGE
  • Median: MEDIAN
  • Min: MIN
  • Max: MAX

When the tool is handy

  • Checking without opening Excel
  • Summarising numbers in notes or emails
  • Looking at only the numbers you selected
  • Pasting the result into text

Seeing it in Excel itself

Selecting a range shows sum, average and count at the bottom of Excel's window (status bar). This tool also shows median, min and max.

Pasting several columns

Pasting two or more columns summarises all numbers together. To get each column, paste them one at a time.

उदाहरण

Copied 12 months of sales and pasted. The total, average and the highest month were clear at once.

Common stumbling blocks

  • Copied formula cells: result values are pasted.
  • Numbers stored as text: numbers treated as text like "'123" are usually read once pasted.
  • Dates mixed in: dates can't be read as numbers. "Non-numbers were skipped." appears.

Frequently asked questions

Are hidden rows summarised?

Only copied ranges count. Rows hidden by a filter aren't copied.
