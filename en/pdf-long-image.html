How to use

  1. Tap "Choose a PDF" and select one PDF.
  2. Choose the image width, format and gap between pages.
  3. Tap "Create the long image".
  4. Save the image.

How it works

  • All pages are joined vertically into one image.
  • If it would be too tall, it is split into several images and bundled in a ZIP.

Notes

  • Many pages mean a large image and a longer wait.
  • Text can no longer be selected or searched.
  • Password-protected PDFs are not supported.

FAQ

Is my PDF uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything is processed inside the browser on your device.

JPG or PNG?

JPG for photo-heavy pages; PNG for text and graphics that need to look crisp.

Why is it split into several images?

Phone browsers limit the height of a single image.
