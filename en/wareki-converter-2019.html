Is 2019 Heisei or Reiwa?

Published: Oct 19, 2026 | Updated: Oct 19, 2026 | About (Japanese)

Key points
  • Heisei until April 30
  • Reiwa from May 1
  • Decide by date

2019 is a year spanning both Heisei and Reiwa. When unsure whether "2019 is Heisei or Reiwa", Convert Between Western Years and Japanese Eras can tell you.

The answer

It differs by month in 2019.

  • 1 January – 30 April 2019: Heisei 31
  • 1 May – 31 December 2019: Reiwa 1

Heisei ended on 30 April 2019 and Reiwa began on 1 May.

What the tool shows

Entering 2019 in "Western year → Japanese era" shows "A year in which the era changed partway". Consider the month and decide which to use.

Steps

  1. Choose "Western year → Japanese era".
  2. Enter 2019 in "Western year".
  3. Check the display and use them according to the month.
When you know the month and day as well as the year, judge by the month and day.

Examples of using them

  • Graduated in March 2019 → graduated Heisei 31, March
  • 30 April 2019 → Heisei 31, 30 April
  • 1 May 2019 → Reiwa 1, 1 May
  • Joined a company in October 2019 → joined Reiwa 1, October

Other years when the era changed

  • 1989: Showa 64 until 7 January, Heisei 1 from 8 January
  • 1926: Taisho 15 until 24 December, Showa 1 from 25 December
  • 1912: Meiji 45 until 29 July, Taisho 1 from 30 July

Cautions on résumés and documents

  • Without the month, you may not be able to decide the era
  • Check the month and write the correct era
  • If the destination specifies Western years, just write Western years (no need to hesitate)

How to write "Reiwa gannen"

Writing "Reiwa gannen" is common rather than "Reiwa 1".

Example

Enrolled April 2019 → Heisei 31, April. Joined May 2019 → Reiwa 1, May.

Common stumbling blocks

  • Judged by year alone: it changes by month.
  • Wrote "Heisei 31, May": from May onward it is Reiwa 1.
  • Western years are easier: if there is no specification, you may unify in Western years.

Frequently asked questions

If I write all of 2019 in one era?

The whole year cannot be expressed in one. Write the month too, or use the Western year to be sure.
