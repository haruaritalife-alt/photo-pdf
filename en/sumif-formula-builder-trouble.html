What to Do When It Does Not Work

Published: Oct 19, 2026 | Updated: Oct 19, 2026 | About (Japanese)

Key points
  • Check the file
  • Check the size
  • Try another device

You built the formula but the result is odd. Here are common troubles with Build a SUMIF or COUNTIF Formula and fixes.

Errors on the tool side

  • "The input is not valid.": the range isn't in the "A2:A100" form, or the condition is empty. SUMIF and AVERAGEIF also need the sum or average range.

Troubles after pasting the formula

  • Result is 0: no cells meet the condition. Often full-width vs half-width differences, extra spaces or notation variation.
  • #VALUE! appears: range sizes don't match (SUMIFS etc.).
  • #DIV/0! appears: AVERAGEIF with no matching cells.
  • #NAME? appears: the function name is wrong.
  • Numbers aren't summed: the numbers in the sum range are text.
  • Date conditions don't work: the dates are text.

Steps (when it doesn't go well)

  1. Check the condition text and the cell text are exactly the same.
  2. Check full-width/half-width and extra spaces.
  3. Check range sizes match.
  4. Check the sum range holds numbers.
If numbers display left-aligned, they may be text.

When numbers are text

  • Select the cells and choose "Convert to Number" from the green triangle mark
  • Or enter 1 in a spare cell, copy it, select the targets and use "Paste Special" with "Multiply"

Special characters in conditions

"*" and "?" are wildcards. To search for them as characters, prefix with "~".

  • Example: "~*" searches for the character "*"

Example

Totalling by "Tokyo" gave 0. The cell was "Tokyo " with a trailing space. Removing it gave the total.

Common stumbling blocks

  • Range lacks $: ranges shift when the formula is copied. Fix with $.
  • Including the header row: including the header in the range is usually fine, but misaligns with the sum range.

Frequently asked questions

Is it case-sensitive?

No. "A" and "a" are treated the same.

Summary

Text matches, range sizes, number form. Check these three.
