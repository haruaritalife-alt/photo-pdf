How to use

  1. Enter the payer (person or company) and the amount, tax included.
  2. Check the tax line, the description and the date.
  3. Enter the issuer (your name or group). Add an address or registration number only if needed.
  4. Tap "Create receipt" and save the PDF. You can email or print it.

Notes

  • Issue a receipt only for money you actually received. A receipt that does not match the facts can be against the law.
  • The result is an A4 PDF. The text is stored as an image.
  • Revenue stamps and invoice rules depend on the situation. If unsure, ask your tax office or a tax accountant.

FAQ

Is what I type uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything is processed inside the browser on your device. Even with "Remember", it stays only on this device.

What if I leave the payer blank?

It will say 上様 ("To whom it may concern").

Can I copy text from the PDF?

No. The text is stored as an image.
