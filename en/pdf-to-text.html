Extract Text from PDF

Get a PDF's text in a form you can copy or save.

Your PDF is never uploaded. Everything is processed on this device.

How to use

  1. Tap "Choose a PDF" and select one PDF.
  2. Choose whether to add page markers, then tap "Extract text".
  3. Copy the text, or save it as a ".txt" file.

Notes

  • It works on PDFs that contain real text, such as ones made from Word.
  • A PDF made by scanning paper is just images, so there is no text to extract. It needs OCR (text recognition).
  • In PDFs with tables or columns, the order and line breaks may differ from the original.

FAQ

Is my PDF uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything is processed inside the browser on your device.

Can I use a password-protected PDF?

No. Please remove the password first.

The text comes out garbled.

Some PDFs store their text in a special way that can't be extracted correctly.
