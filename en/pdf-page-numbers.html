How to use

  1. Tap "Choose a PDF" and select one PDF.
  2. Pick the position, the style ("1", "1 / 10" or "- 1 -") and the size.
  3. Tap "Add page numbers" and save.

When it helps

  • You want page numbers on a report or document PDF
  • You want the cover unnumbered and counting to start from page 2 (set "First page" to "Skip it")
  • You want the numbers to continue from another document (change "Number of page 1")

FAQ

Is my PDF uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything is processed inside the browser on your device.

Do the text or quality change?

No. Only the numbers are added on top of the original pages.

Can a number overlap text?

In PDFs with narrow margins it can. If so, try a different position.
