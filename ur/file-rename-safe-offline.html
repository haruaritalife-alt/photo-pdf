فائلوں کو اپ لوڈ کیے بغیر ان کے نام بدلنا

شائع شدہ: 19 اکتوبر 2026 | اپ ڈیٹ: 19 اکتوبر 2026 | تعارف (جاپانی)

اہم نکات
  • فائلیں چنیں
  • نام بدلیں
  • محفوظ کریں

Uploading important files such as contracts, customer data and family photos to an outside service just to rename them is worrying. Rename Many Files at Once (Numbers, Dates) renames inside the browser without sending files outside.

یہ کیسے کام کرتا ہے

  • The files you choose are loaded only inside your browser.
  • Both renaming and making the ZIP happen on your device.
  • Neither the files nor the resulting ZIP are sent to a server or stored.

مراحل

  1. Choose files with "Choose files".
  2. Decide the name rule.
  3. Press "Rename and save as ZIP".
Files are processed inside this device. They are sent nowhere.

How to confirm

  • It works even with the connection cut (airplane mode).
  • Browser developer tools can confirm that no file is sent.

Still be careful about

  • The ZIP made: an ordinary file with no password. Mind where it's kept and how widely it's shared.
  • The original files: they remain as they are. Tidy them yourself if not needed.
  • Shared computers: when finished, delete the files and ZIP.
  • Cloud-synced folders: check that the save location isn't uploaded automatically.
  • Content of file names: take care not to put personal names or secrets in names.

Uses it suits

  • Unifying file names of customer material and contracts
  • Organising family photos
  • Checking file names before sending outside

مثال

Wanted to align 100 files for a client to rule-following names. Didn't want to upload them to an outside service, but since it's processed in the browser, it could be used with peace of mind.

Common stumbling blocks

  • The result disappeared when closing the page: by design nothing is saved. Save before closing.
  • Heavy with large files: divide into several rounds.

Frequently asked questions

Can the operator see the files?

No. Files are not sent outside.

خلاصہ

Files are not sent outside. Mind where the ZIP is kept and the content of names.

«بہت سی فائلوں کے نام ایک ساتھ بدلیں (نمبر، تاریخ)» استعمال کریں (مفت)

وہ ٹولز جو اس کے ساتھ اچھے لگتے ہیں

  • ZIP فائل بنائیں: کئی فائلوں کو ایک ZIP میں پیک کریں۔
  • ZIP فائل کھولیں (ان زپ): ZIP کے اندر کا مواد دیکھیں اور ضروری فائلیں نکالیں۔
  • تصاویر کا سائز پکسلز میں تبدیل کریں: تصاویر کا سائز چوڑائی، اونچائی یا فیصد کے مطابق بدلیں۔

اگر یہ گائیڈ مفید رہی تو ایک چھوٹی سی ٹپ ہمارے لیے بہت معنی رکھتی ہے۔
