بزنس کارڈ کا سائز اور گھر یا اسٹور پر پرنٹ اور کٹنگ کے نکات

شائع شدہ: 19 اکتوبر 2026 | اپ ڈیٹ: 19 اکتوبر 2026 | تعارف (جاپانی)

اہم نکات
  • معیاری کارڈ 91×55 mm کا ہوتا ہے
  • A4 پر اصل سائز (100%) میں پرنٹ کریں
  • رولر اور کٹر یا پیپر ٹرمر سے کاٹیں

Size matters for business cards. If cut off-line they look bad and may not fit a card holder. Here are the card sizes made with Create Business Cards / Name Cards and tips for printing at home or at a convenience store and cutting.

The sizes in this tool

  • Business cards: 91×55 mm, 10 per A4 page. The most widely used size in Japan.
  • Name tags: 100×70 mm, 8 per A4 page.

پرنٹ سیٹنگز

  1. کاغذ کو A4 پر رکھیں۔
  2. Set the scale to "Actual size". Do not use shrink settings such as "Fit to page", since they change the size.
  3. Set margins to "Standard" or "None". Some printers have a non-printable area.

ٹیسٹ پرنٹ کریں

First test-print one sheet on plain paper and measure the size with a ruler. If it is 91×55 mm, it is fine.

Tips for cutting

  • If you make it with "Draw light cut lines", you get a guide for where to cut.
  • A ruler and cutter, or a paper trimmer, cuts cleanly.
  • With scissors, cut into strips first, then across.
  • Decide whether to cut on the line or inside it, and be consistent.

کاغذ کا انتخاب

  • Plain paper: suited to test prints.
  • Thick paper or business-card paper: makes sturdier cards.
  • When using perforated business-card sheets, check with a test print that the slots line up.

کنوینیئنس اسٹور پر پرنٹنگ

  1. Save the PDF.
  2. At the store's multi-copy machine, you can print from a USB drive, an app and so on (the method differs by store).
  3. Choose A4 as the paper size and "Actual size" as the scale.
The store's settings screen may default to "enlarge/reduce". Always check.

Common stumbling blocks

  • The size is different: check the scale is not "Fit to page".
  • The edges are cut off: the printer has a non-printable margin. Review the margin setting.
  • It shifts when cut: confirm with a test print and cut along the light lines.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a Western size?

The card size in this tool is 91×55 mm.

Can I print on both sides?

This tool makes single-sided cards.

خلاصہ

Print on A4 at actual size, test first, and cut along the light lines for clean cards.
