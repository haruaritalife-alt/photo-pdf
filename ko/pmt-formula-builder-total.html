이자 합계

게시: 2026년 10월 19일 | 업데이트: 2026년 10월 19일 | 소개 (일본어)

핵심 포인트
  • TOTALINT를 고릅니다
  • 금액 입력
  • 복사

Taking a loan, how much will you pay in the end? How much total interest? "TOTAL" and "TOTALINT" in Build a PMT Formula give the total repaid and the total interest.

순서

  1. Enter the amount, annual rate, term and payments per year.
  2. Choose "TOTAL" or "TOTALINT" in "Type".
  3. Copy the formula and paste.
TOTAL is the total repaid (principal plus interest). TOTALINT is the interest only.

예

Borrowing 3,000,000 yen at 1.5% annual for 10 years, monthly:

  • TOTAL: =-PMT(1.5%/12,10*12,3000000)*10*12 → about 3.23 million yen
  • TOTALINT: =-PMT(1.5%/12,10*12,3000000)*10*12-3000000 → about 230,000 yen

작동 방식

  • Total = payment × number of payments
  • Total interest = total − amount borrowed

Comparing terms

Even for the same amount, a longer term means more interest.

  • 10 years: less interest, higher monthly payment
  • 20 years: more interest, lower monthly payment

Line up several terms to compare.

Comparing rates

Even a 0.5% rate difference changes the total a lot. Change the rate field and compare the sample calculations.

알맞은 경우

  • Comparing loan conditions
  • Rough guides for considering early repayment
  • Grasping the total cost of borrowing

예

Torn between 10 and 15 years. Computing total interest for each showed the balance between monthly payment and total.

Common stumbling blocks

  • Differs from actual payments: fees and guarantee charges aren't included.
  • Variable rates: if the rate changes midway, so does the total.

Frequently asked questions

Does it work for equal-principal repayment?

PMT calculates equal-installment (principal and interest) repayment. It doesn't fit equal-principal repayment.

요약

Total and interest come from PMT × count. Compare by changing the term.
