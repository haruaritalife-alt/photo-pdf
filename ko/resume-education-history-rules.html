일본식 이력서에 학력과 경력 쓰는 방법

게시: 2026년 10월 19일 | 업데이트: 2026년 10월 19일 | 소개 (일본어)

핵심 포인트
  • 학력은 보통 고등학교 입학부터 씁니다
  • 학교와 회사 이름은 정식 명칭으로 쓰기
  • 오른쪽에 以上으로 마무리하기

The education and work history section of a résumé has several writing rules. Without knowing them, you hesitate over how to write, or give a worse impression. Following the input fields of Create a Resume PDF (Japanese Rirekisho), here are the basics.

Three basic rules

  1. Write school and company names in full: not an abbreviation, but the full official name, such as "Co., Ltd." in full.
  2. Keep the years consistent: use either the Western calendar or the Japanese era calendar and do not switch midway.
  3. Close with "That is all" (以上): write it on the right side of the row after the last row of work history.

Writing education

  • In the centre of the first row, write "Education".
  • From the next row, write each entry and graduation, one per row.
  • Example: Apr 2019 Entered X High School, Mar 2022 Graduated from X High School
  • Starting from high school is usual. If you went to university or a vocational school, write their entry and graduation too.

Writing work history

  • In the centre of the row after education, write "Work history".
  • From the next row, write each joining and leaving, one per row.
  • Example: Apr 2022 Joined X Co., Ltd., Mar 2025 Left for personal reasons
  • If you are still working there, write "to the present".
  • Include part-time jobs only when needed.

If you have no work history

Leaving it blank puts "None". After that, add "That is all" at the end of education.

The row limit

In this tool, education and work history are limited to 16 rows including headings and "That is all". If there are too many, reduce rows.

  • Do not combine entry and graduation in one row
  • Include only part-time jobs relevant to the company
  • Keep older work history short

순서

  1. Under "Education and work history", enter the year, month and content row by row.
  2. Add rows with "+ Add row" if needed.
  3. Check the order and notation in the preview.
The input example takes the form "2019 / 4 / Entered X High School". Write school names in full.

Common stumbling blocks

  • "... is too long" appears: shorten the row, or split it into rows.
  • Years are not consistent: unify to either Western or Japanese era years.
  • You forgot the "Education" and "Work history" headings: headings count as rows too.

Frequently asked questions

Do I start from elementary or junior high school?

Usually from high school. Follow the company's instructions.

Should I give the reason for leaving in detail?

Keep it brief, such as "left for personal reasons" or "left due to the company's circumstances".

요약

Write in full, keep the years consistent and close with "That is all". Following these three makes a neat résumé.
