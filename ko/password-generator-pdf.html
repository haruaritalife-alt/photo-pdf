PDF 비밀번호로 쓸 때의 주의점

게시: 2026년 10월 19일 | 업데이트: 2026년 10월 19일 | 소개 (일본어)

핵심 포인트
  • 비밀번호 생성
  • PDF에 설정하기
  • 따로 전달

When putting a password on a PDF, a random, hard-to-guess password is recommended. Here are cautions for using a password made with Generate a Strong Random Password on a PDF.

순서

  1. Decide the "Length". 12–16 characters is recommended for a PDF.
  2. Ticking "Exclude confusable characters (0 and O, 1 and l, etc.)" makes it easier to convey.
  3. Press "Make" and copy with "Copy".
  4. Paste into "Password" and "Password (confirm)" of the tool that sets a PDF password.
  5. Be sure to record the password before saving the PDF.
If you forget a PDF's password, that PDF can no longer be opened. Save the password you made before closing the screen.

Characteristics of PDF passwords

  • You need to convey it to the other party (email, phone and so on).
  • The other party may type it by hand.
  • Full-width/half-width differences can make it impossible to open.

권장 설정

  • Length: around 12 characters (too long is hard for the other party to type)
  • Exclude confusable characters: on
  • Symbols: depending on the other party's environment they are hard to type, so you may leave them out.
  • Character types: uppercase, lowercase, digits

How to convey it

  • Do not write the password in the same email as the PDF.
  • Convey it by another route, such as a phone call or a separate email.
  • It is kind to mention that all of it is half-width.

How to record it

  • Record the PDF name and password in a manager app
  • Write it on paper and keep it in a safe place
  • Do not put the password in the same folder as the PDF

예

Made a 12-character password to send a quotation to a business partner. Used "Exclude confusable characters" and told it by phone. No mishearing occurred.

Common stumbling blocks

  • The other party cannot open it: ask them to check full-width/half-width and case.
  • Forgot to save the password: it disappears when the screen is closed. If you make it again, remake the PDF too.

Frequently asked questions

Should I change the password each time?

Changing it per important document is safer.

요약

Make it around 12 characters, excluding confusable ones. Convey it by another route and do not forget to record it.
