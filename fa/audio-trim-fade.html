جلوگیری از صدای تق در نقاط برش

منتشرشده: 19 اکتبر 2026 | به‌روزرسانی: 19 اکتبر 2026 | دربارهٔ ما (ژاپنی)

نکات کلیدی
  • گزینه محو شدن را تیک بزنید
  • ذخیره
  • برای بررسی گوش دهید

Cutting audio midway can produce a "pop" at the cut, because the waveform stops abruptly. The "Soften the start and end slightly" option in Trim an Audio File prevents this.

مراحل

  1. Under "Choose audio", select an audio file.
  2. Enter "Start (sec)" and "End (sec)".
  3. Tick "Soften the start and end slightly".
  4. Press "Cut and save" and save with "Save WAV".
The volume rises gradually at the start and falls gradually at the end.

Why noise appears

Sound has the shape of a wave. Cutting abruptly mid-wave changes the sound instantly, heard as a "pop". Raising and lowering the volume smoothly makes this unnoticeable.

Where it helps most

  • Cutting music or ambient sound
  • Making ringtones or sound effects
  • When joining with other audio

Where it helps less

  • When cutting at an already silent place
  • When cutting at a quiet break in speech

Tips for cut positions

  • Cut at breaks in speech or breaths
  • Avoid moments when the sound is loud
  • Choose places near silence

مناسب برای

  • Natural cuts that are easy to listen to
  • Playing several audio clips one after another

مثال

After cutting part of a song there was a "pop" at the end. Ticking "Soften the start and end slightly" and redoing it made it vanish naturally.

Common stumbling blocks

  • Start sounds quiet: because of the softening, the first instant is lower in volume. If bothersome, start a little earlier.
  • Hard to notice the effect: the softening is short, so the change isn't large.

Frequently asked questions

Can the softening length be changed?

In this tool it's a fixed setting that softens "slightly".

خلاصه

If noise in a cut sound bothers you, use "Soften the start and end slightly" for more listenable audio.

استفاده از «برش فایل صوتی» (رایگان)

ابزارهایی که با این ابزار خوب هماهنگ‌اند

  • ذخیره فریم‌های ویدیو به‌صورت تصویر: هر لحظه از ویدیو را به‌صورت تصویر ذخیره کنید.
  • تبدیل کدگذاری متن (رفع متن خراب): فایل‌های متنی را بین Shift_JIS، UTF-8، EUC-JP و غیره تبدیل کنید تا متن خراب درست شود.
  • تغییر نام گروهی فایل‌ها (شماره، تاریخ): تغییر نام چندین فایل با شماره یا تاریخ.

اگر این راهنما مفید بود، یک انعام کوچک برایمان ارزش زیادی دارد.
