انتخاب نسبت ابعاد: 1:1، 4:3، 3:2، 16:9 و 3:4

منتشرشده: 19 اکتبر 2026 | به‌روزرسانی: 19 اکتبر 2026 | دربارهٔ ما (ژاپنی)

نکات کلیدی
  • 1:1 مربع است؛ 4:3 و 3:2 شکل عکس‌اند؛ 16:9 برای صفحه‌نمایش و ویدیو است
  • 3:4 برای پرتره‌های عمودی رایج است
  • اگر مطمئن نیستید، از راهنمای گیرنده یا پلتفرم پیروی کنید

When cropping an image you can choose a "Ratio". There are many, such as 1:1, 4:3, 3:2, 16:9 and 3:4, and it is hard to know which to choose. Here is a guide to using the "Ratio" options of Crop & Rotate Image.

What a ratio is

It is the proportion of an image's width to its height. For example, 4:3 is width 4 to height 3, a slightly wide shape. When you set a ratio and crop, the area moves while keeping that shape. With "Free" you can cut any shape.

Features and uses of each

  • 1:1 (square): social media icons, profile pictures, product photo lists.
  • 4:3: the standard ratio of older TVs and many cameras. Often used for slides and blog images.
  • 3:2: close to DSLR and photo prints (such as L size). A balanced, photographic shape.
  • 16:9: the wide ratio of today's TVs, video and most slides. Suits header images and video thumbnails.
  • 3:4: the portrait version of 4:3. Suits vertical phone photos and tall posters.
  • Free: when there is no set ratio.

A guide by purpose

  • Social media icon: 1:1
  • Blog featured image: 16:9 or 3:2
  • Photo for a slide: 16:9 or 4:3
  • Photo prints: 3:2
  • Tall images for phones: 3:4

مراحل

  1. With "Choose image", select the image.
  2. Under "Ratio", choose the ratio you want.
  3. Drag the area to move it.
  4. Press "Crop" and save.
If the recipient specifies a ratio, follow it.

چگونه تصمیم بگیریم

  • Match the shape of the frame where it will be used.
  • If unsure, choosing one close to the original photo's ratio means less is cut away.
  • Cropping a portrait photo to a wide ratio cuts off a lot at the top and bottom.

Common stumbling blocks

  • It does not come out the shape you wanted: check that the ratio is not on "Free".
  • An important part got cut off: move the position of the area.
  • The image got smaller: matching a ratio cuts away part, so it gets smaller.

Frequently asked questions

Can I change the ratio later?

You can crop again, but the part already cut off does not come back. Starting again from the original image is the surest way.

Can I specify pixel dimensions?

This tool decides by ratio. Adjust pixel dimensions with the image resizing tool.

خلاصه

Choose the ratio to suit where it will be used. When unsure, a ratio close to the original photo is the safe choice.
