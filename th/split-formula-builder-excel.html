TEXTSPLIT ใน Excel

เผยแพร่: 19 ต.ค. 2026 | อัปเดต: 19 ต.ค. 2026 | เกี่ยวกับ (ภาษาญี่ปุ่น)

ประเด็นสำคัญ
  • สร้างสูตร
  • เลือกอันที่สอง
  • วางรหัส

SPLIT is a Google Sheets function. Excel has TEXTSPLIT. Build a SPLIT Formula builds both formulas at once.

The Excel formula

  • Google: =SPLIT(A2,",")
  • Excel: =TEXTSPLIT(A2,",")

In the tool, "Copy the second" copies the Excel formula.

TEXTSPLIT is available in newer Excel such as 365, not in older versions.

Supported versions

  • Microsoft 365: yes
  • Excel 2021: may not
  • Excel 2019 and earlier: no

Alternatives in old Excel

In older versions, you can split by:

  • Using Data tab → Text to Columns (good for one-off work)
  • Combining LEFT, MID and FIND

เหมาะกับ

  • Using the same sheet in both Google and Excel
  • Knowing how to rewrite when moving formulas

ตัวอย่าง

Moved a Google-built sheet to Excel. SPLIT errored, so I switched to the second TEXTSPLIT formula and it worked.

Common stumbling blocks

  • #NAME?: your Excel doesn't support TEXTSPLIT.
  • #SPILL!: values sit in the spill range.

Frequently asked questions

What about multiple delimiters?

Google lets you list delimiter characters together. Excel takes an array. Edit the formula by hand.

สรุป

In Excel, use TEXTSPLIT in place of SPLIT.
