Combining several pages per sheet and printing double-sided as well cuts paper use sharply. Here are points for printing a PDF made with Combine PDF Pages onto One Sheet (2-up, 4-up) double-sided.

How much paper it saves

For a 40-page PDF:

  • Printed as is, single-sided: 40 sheets
  • 4 per sheet, single-sided: 10 sheets
  • 4 per sheet, double-sided: 5 sheets

Adjust by combining, for example 2 per sheet double-sided gives 10 sheets.

ขั้นตอน

  1. Choose the PDF and choose the number of pages to combine.
  2. Make the PDF with "Combine onto one sheet" and save it.
  3. When printing, choose "Double-sided". Use A4 and "Actual size (100%)".
Double-sided settings differ by printer and by convenience-store machine.

Binding setting

Double-sided printing has "long-edge binding" and "short-edge binding".

  • Portrait paper (4 and 9 pages): long-edge binding. Binding on the left, like a book.
  • Landscape paper (2, 6 and 8 pages): short-edge binding. Binding at the top, like a calendar.

If the setting does not match, the back side comes out upside down.

Show-through

Printing both sides on thin paper lets the back show through and hurts readability.

  • Use slightly thicker paper
  • Set the margin to "Normal" and keep the text toward the centre

A note on margins

If you will bind it, you need a binding margin (the part for punching holes). Setting "Outer margin" to "Normal" gives some room.

ตัวอย่าง

A 50-page seminar handout: 4 per sheet, printed double-sided. It comes to 7 sheets and is easy to staple.

Common stumbling blocks

  • The back side is upside down: review the binding setting (long or short edge).
  • The back shows through: use thicker paper.
  • Double-sided printing is not possible: if the printer does not support it, flip the pages and print manually.

Frequently asked questions

How do I print double-sided manually?

There is a way to print odd and even pages separately. Check the printer's instructions.

What does double-sided printing cost?

Prices differ at places such as convenience stores. Check the store's information.

สรุป

Combine and print double-sided. Watch the binding setting and you can cut paper a lot.
