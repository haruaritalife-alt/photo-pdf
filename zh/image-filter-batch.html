如何一次把多张图片变成黑白

发布日期：2026 年 10 月 19 日｜最后更新：2026 年 10 月 19 日｜关于（日文）

要点
  • 选择多张图片
  • 选择效果
  • 保存为 ZIP

You want to turn many images black and white at once. One by one is tedious. Apply Black-and-White, Sepia and Other Looks can process several images together under the same conditions.

步骤

  1. With "Choose images", select multiple images together.
  2. Choose the "Finish": "Black and white", "Document (crisp black and white)", "Sepia", "Brighter", "Darker".
  3. Choose the "Strength": weak, medium, strong.
  4. Choose the "Format": JPG or PNG.
  5. Press "Change colours and save" and check the "Preview".
  6. Save them together with "Save ZIP".
The same finish and strength are applied to all images. Process those needing different conditions in separate groups.

Using the ZIP

  1. Unzip the saved ZIP.
  2. Use the images inside for printing or sharing.
  3. Sorting by file name restores the original order.

适合

  • Making scanned document images readable in bulk
  • Turning colour images black and white in bulk for monochrome printing (saves ink)
  • Giving album photos a unified tone (black and white or sepia)

When documents and photos are mixed

Documents suit "Document (crisp black and white)" and photos suit "Black and white". Process them in separate groups.

Turning black-and-white images into a PDF

Black-and-white images can be combined into a PDF for printing or submission. Use the tool that turns images into a PDF.

示例

30 scanned documents converted in bulk with "Document (crisp black and white)", medium, PNG. Saved as a ZIP and combined into a PDF.

Common stumbling blocks

  • Only some images failed: check for HEIC or similar formats mixed in.
  • The ZIP is large: use JPG or use the compression tool as well.
  • Only some finishes look wrong: process just that image with a different strength.

Frequently asked questions

How many can I choose?

It depends on your device. If many, process in parts.

小结

Select several, set the conditions and save as a ZIP. Bulk processing under the same conditions.
