用多个画面制作 GIF

发布日期：2026 年 10 月 19 日｜最后更新：2026 年 10 月 19 日｜关于（日文）

要点
  • 以较短的间隔提取
  • 打开 ZIP
  • 制作 GIF

You want to turn part of a video into an animated GIF. One way is to pull consecutive images from the video with Save Video Frames as Images and join them with a GIF-making tool.

Overall flow

  1. Pull images from the video at regular intervals with this tool (ZIP).
  2. Unzip and choose the images to use.
  3. Join them with the "Make an animated GIF from images" tool.

Steps (pulling images)

  1. Choose a video with "Choose video".
  2. Under "How to save", select "At regular intervals, in bulk (ZIP)".
  3. Enter a short number (0.2–0.5, etc.) in "Interval (sec)".
  4. Choosing JPG for "Image format" keeps it light.
  5. Press "Save in bulk (ZIP)" and save with "Save ZIP".
For smooth motion, shorten the interval. But bulk is up to 200 images.

Interval and length

  • Interval 0.5 sec, 20 images → about 10 seconds
  • Interval 0.2 sec, 20 images → about 4 seconds

Decide interval and count to match the GIF length you want.

Steps (making the GIF)

  1. Unzip and check the images to use.
  2. Open "Make an animated GIF from images".
  3. Choose the images, decide display time and make the GIF.

适合

  • Turning step explanations into animated GIFs
  • Lightly sharing a short motion scene
  • Making moving images for social media or blogs

示例

Turned a button-press operation in a screen recording into images every 0.3 seconds. Choosing 20 and making a GIF produced an explanatory GIF where the steps move.

Common stumbling blocks

  • GIF is heavy: many images or large image sizes make it heavy. Reduce the count.
  • Motion is jerky: the interval is long. Shorten it and retake.

Frequently asked questions

Does a video become a GIF in one step?

Two tools are combined. First pull images, then join them into a GIF.

小结

Pull images at short intervals and join them into a GIF. Part of a video becomes a moving image.
