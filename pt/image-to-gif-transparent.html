O que acontece com o fundo de um GIF

Publicado em: 19 out 2026 | Atualizado em: 19 out 2026 | Sobre (em japonês)

Pontos principais
  • Confira o tamanho da primeira imagem
  • Confira as margens
  • Se preciso, iguale os tamanhos antes

What happens to the background when you make a GIF from PNG images with transparent backgrounds? In the GIF made with Make an Animated GIF from Images, care is needed over how transparent parts are handled.

Basic idea

When an image with a transparent background becomes a GIF, the transparent parts may turn into a background colour such as white. If you want to keep transparency, check the result.

Passos

  1. Choose the images and check the order.
  2. Press "Make GIF" and look at the preview.
  3. Check that the background is as intended.
  4. If fine, save.
GIF is limited to 256 colours, so semi-transparent edges may not be rendered cleanly.

Characteristics of GIF transparency

  • GIF transparency is a binary: transparent or opaque
  • Semi-transparency (faintly see-through) cannot be represented
  • Boundaries may look jagged

Dicas para um resultado limpo

  • If using it where the background colour is fixed, prepare images with that colour as the background
  • If putting it on a white-background page, a white background is fine
  • Blend transparent edges into the background colour beforehand

When you want to add a background

If you want to give the image a different background colour, add it first with an image editing tool. Or tidy the background with a background-transparency tool before using.

Bom para

  • Animation of logos and icons
  • Moving material for pages with a fixed background

Differences by where it's used

  • White-background sites: white is fine
  • Coloured-background sites: use images matched to the background colour
  • Social media: the background may automatically become white or black

Exemplo

Joined 3 transparent-background logos into a GIF. It was going on a white site, so I used the white background as it was, and it displayed cleanly.

Common stumbling blocks

  • A white line appears at the edge: the effect of semi-transparent parts. Make images matched to the background colour.
  • The background turns black: how the image's transparent parts are handled. Check in the preview.

Frequently asked questions

Can a fully transparent GIF be made?

It depends. Check in the preview and, if needed, use it with a background colour.

Resumo

GIF transparency is binary. Matching the background colour of where you use it is the most reliable.
