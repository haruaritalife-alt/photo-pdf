Como escolher opacidade, tamanho e cor: equilíbrio entre visibilidade e legibilidade

Publicado em: 19 out 2026 | Atualizado em: 19 out 2026 | Sobre (em japonês)

Pontos principais
  • Comece com opacidade "Média"
  • Mire em cerca de metade da página
  • Cinza é a cor segura

A watermark that is too strong makes the body unreadable, and one too faint goes unnoticed. Here is how to decide "Strength", "Size", "Color" and "Placement" in Add a Text Watermark to PDF, balanced against readability.

Intensidade

  • Light: the body is easy to read. The watermark is restrained.
  • Strong: the watermark is clearly visible. The body can become hard to read.

Try "light" first and use "strong" if it goes unnoticed.

Tamanho

  • Small: unlikely to get in the way. Hard to notice.
  • Normal: standard.
  • Large: conspicuous, but easily overlaps the body.

Cor

  • Gray: a safe colour that suits any document.
  • Red: draws strong attention. For "Sample", "DRAFT" and so on.
  • Azul: uma impressão tranquila.
  • Black: clearly visible but mixes with the body. Easier to use combined with "light".

Posição

  • One large in the centre: overlaps the whole document. Strongly shows the purpose.
  • Repeat across the page: even if cropped, it remains somewhere. But the text multiplies, so the body is more likely to become hard to read.
  • Small at the lower right: little effect on the body. Hard to notice.

Direção

  • Diagonal: the standard. Even when it overlaps the body, it is easy to tell apart.
  • Horizontal: same direction as the lines, readable, but mixes with the body.

Example combinations

  • Prioritising readability: gray, light, diagonal, one large in the centre.
  • Prioritising deterrence: gray, light, diagonal, repeat across the page.
  • Not affecting the body: gray, light, horizontal, small at the lower right.
  • Emphasis: red, strong, diagonal, one large in the centre (the body becomes hard to read, so take care).

Passos

  1. First make it with the standard combination (gray, light, diagonal, one large in the centre).
  2. Check in the "Preview of page 1" that the body can be read.
  3. If it is hard to notice, raise the strength or size.
  4. If it is hard to read, make it lighter and smaller.
On pages with tables or a lot of small text, check especially that overlaps do not make it unreadable.

Common stumbling blocks

  • The body cannot be read: set "light" and make the size "small" or "normal".
  • The watermark is almost invisible: set "strong", or make the colour "red" or "black".
  • "Repeat across the page" is noisy: change to "One large in the centre".

Frequently asked questions

Might a faint watermark vanish when printed?

Depending on the printer, pale colours may not come out well. If you will print, confirm with a test print.

Does the preview of page 1 show how all pages look?

The overlap changes with each page's content. It is reassuring to open other pages after saving.

Resumo

Gray, light, diagonal is the basis. Check in the preview that the body is readable and adjust little by little if it is not enough.
