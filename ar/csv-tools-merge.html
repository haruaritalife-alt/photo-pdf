دمج عدة ملفات CSV في ملف واحد

تاريخ النشر: 19 أكتوبر 2026 | آخر تحديث: 19 أكتوبر 2026 | حول (باليابانية)

النقاط الرئيسية
  • اختر عدة ملفات CSV
  • اختر "دمج في ملف واحد"
  • احفظ

You want to combine CSVs split by month or store into one. Copy-and-paste makes headers overlap or drops rows. "Merge" in Merge, Split and Pick Columns from CSV combines them safely in one go.

الخطوات

  1. With "Choose CSV files", choose all the CSVs to merge.
  2. Under "What to do", choose "Merge (combine into one)".
  3. Tick "First row is a header" (if there are headers).
  4. Check "Beginning of content" and press "Run and save".
For CSVs with headers, the header row goes in only once, without overlapping.

Check before merging

  • Do all CSVs have the same number and order of columns?
  • Are header names consistent?
  • Are the encodings not wildly different?

Merging CSVs with different column structures shifts the data.

Handling of headers

  • With "First row is a header": the first CSV's header stays and other CSVs' header rows are removed
  • Without it: all rows line up one after another as they are

ترتيب الدمج

CSVs connect in the order chosen. For month order, choose from January onward.

Checks after merging

  • Does the row count match the total?
  • Are headers not overlapping?
  • Are dates and amounts intact?

مناسب لـ

  • Combining monthly data into annual
  • Combining store data into company-wide
  • Restoring data that was exported in pieces

مثال

Wanted to combine sales CSVs from 10 stores into one. Selecting all and running "Merge" produced a CSV for all stores with the header only once.

Common stumbling blocks

  • Columns shift: column structures differ among CSVs. Align them first.
  • Headers overlap: "First row is a header" wasn't ticked.
  • Over 30MB: merge in parts or narrow the columns.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a limit on the number of CSVs to merge?

The overall size is limited to 30MB.

الخلاصة

Align the column structure, check "First row is a header" and merge.
