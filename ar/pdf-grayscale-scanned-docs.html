تحويل المستندات الممسوحة ضوئيًا إلى أبيض وأسود: سهولة القراءة مقابل الحجم

تاريخ النشر: 19 أكتوبر 2026 | آخر تحديث: 19 أكتوبر 2026 | حول (باليابانية)

النقاط الرئيسية
  • للظلال والثنيات، استخدم الأبيض والأسود الخالص
  • للمستندات التي فيها صور، استخدم التدرج الرمادي
  • صوّر مستقيمًا وبإضاءة جيدة

Scanned documents can have yellowed backgrounds or shadows, and are hard to read if printed as is. Turning them black and white with Convert a PDF to Grayscale (Black & White) makes it easier to balance readability and size.

Features of scanned documents

  • The paper looks gray or yellowish
  • Shadows appear at the corners and edges
  • The whole thing prints dark
  • They are often already in image form (a PDF whose text cannot be selected)

الإعدادات الموصى بها

For text-centred documents (contracts, applications, invoices) these settings suit.

  • Type: pure black and white (crisp text)
  • Resolution: normal (150 dpi). If the text is small, fine (200 dpi).

For documents with photos, or where seal impressions and figures matter, try "Grayscale (with shades)".

الخطوات

  1. With "Choose a PDF", choose the scanned PDF.
  2. Choose "Pure black and white (crisp text)".
  3. Set the resolution to "normal (150 dpi)".
  4. Press "Make black and white" and check the preview.
  5. If the background becomes white and the text is crisp, it worked.
  6. Save and check all pages.
Faint text, fine seal impressions and pencil notes can vanish in pure black and white.

Balancing with size

  • Pure black and white tends to be smaller for scanned documents
  • Grayscale looks nicer but is larger
  • If the recipient has a limit, adjust the resolution or use the compress tool

If the original scan is an image PDF

A scanned document is already an image, so its text cannot be selected. Turning it black and white with this tool does not make the text searchable or selectable. If you want the text, use the text recognition (OCR) tool.

مثال

A scan (in colour) of a contract: pure black and white at 150 dpi gave a clean background and a smaller size.

Common stumbling blocks

  • Text vanishes: raise the resolution or use grayscale.
  • Shadows remain: pure black and white may make shadows less noticeable.
  • The page orientation is wrong: fix it first with the rotate tool.

Frequently asked questions

What if the scan quality is poor?

Rescanning brighter and straighter is more reliable.

What should I do before submitting?

Open all pages and check that they are readable.

الخلاصة

For scanned documents, pure black and white at 150 dpi is the basis. Check that no text has vanished before using it.
