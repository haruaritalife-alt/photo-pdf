كيفية دمج الصور وملفات PDF في ملف واحد

تاريخ النشر: 19 أكتوبر 2026 | آخر تحديث: 19 أكتوبر 2026 | حول (باليابانية)

النقاط الرئيسية
  • حوّل الصور إلى PDF أولًا بواسطة «الصور إلى PDF»
  • ثم ادمج ملف PDF هذا مع الملفات الأخرى في «دمج ملفات PDF»

You have a mix of PDFs and photos and want them in one file. This tool joins PDFs to PDFs, so the photos are turned into a PDF first. You combine Merge PDFs with the tool that turns photos into PDF.

The overall flow

  1. Convert the photos to a PDF.
  2. Merge that PDF with the PDFs you already have.
  3. Check the order and save.

الخطوات

Turn the photos into a PDF

In "Photos to PDF", choose the photos, set the order and make the PDF. Fix any wrongly oriented photo with "Rotate".

Merge the PDFs

  1. With "Choose PDFs", select the PDF made from photos and the other PDFs.
  2. Arrange the "Merge order".
  3. Press "Merge" and save.
If you want the page size (for example A4) to match, choose "A4" when you make the PDF from photos.

When there are many photos

Dozens of photos make the file heavy. Adjust with the quality setting or the size limit when you turn the photos into a PDF.

نصائح للمقروئية

  • Keep photos in the same orientation as the documents (portrait).
  • For small items such as receipts, a setting that fits them onto A4 works well.
  • With many pages, adding a contents or list page at the front makes it easier to follow.

Common stumbling blocks

  • Only the photos have a different orientation: rotate them when you turn the photos into a PDF.
  • Page sizes do not match: making them at A4 keeps them uniform after merging.
  • The text is hard to read: set the quality to "High" and retake the photo.

Frequently asked questions

Can I choose photos and PDFs together from the start?

This tool takes only PDFs. Turn the photos into a PDF first.

Does quality drop when converting and merging?

Not when merging. It depends on the quality setting used during conversion.

الخلاصة

Turn the photos into a PDF first, then merge it with the other PDFs. If the size and orientation match, you get one easy-to-read file.
