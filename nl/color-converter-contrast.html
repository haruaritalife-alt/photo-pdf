Contrast tussen tekst en achtergrond

Gepubliceerd: 19 okt 2026 | Bijgewerkt: 19 okt 2026 | Over ons (in het Japans)

Belangrijkste punten
  • Voer de achtergrondkleur in
  • Bekijk het contrast
  • Kies de tekstkleur

A poor combination of text colour and background colour makes text hard to read. Have you ever put light grey text on a white background and found it unreadable? Color Code Converter (HEX, RGB, HSL) shows a colour's brightness difference from white and black. It's a guide to readability.

Stappen

  1. Enter the colour to check (text colour or background colour) in the "Colour code" field.
  2. Look at the "Brightness difference from white" and "from black" shown on screen.
  3. Choose the one with the larger difference as its partner.
The greater the brightness difference, the more readable the combination.

Hoe je het leest

  • Large difference from white: white text on this colour is easy to read.
  • Large difference from black: black text on this colour is easy to read.
  • Small differences from both: a mid-tone colour. Take care choosing the text colour.

Handleiding

The brightness difference (contrast ratio) is a value from 1 to 21.

  • 4.5 or more: the recommended standard for ordinary text
  • 3 or more: the recommended standard for large text
  • 7 or more: a higher standard

Veelgemaakte fouten

  • Light grey text on a white background
  • Yellow text on a white background
  • Red text on a blue background

Depending on the combination, it can look stylish but be hard to read.

Steps (deciding colours)

  1. Decide the background colour.
  2. Enter the background colour and see whether the difference from white or black is larger.
  3. Make the colour with the larger difference the text colour.
  4. Check readability on an actual screen.

Why it matters

  • It becomes readable for older people and those with weaker eyesight
  • It stays readable in bright places (phones outdoors)
  • A basis of accessibility (being usable by everyone)

Goed voor

  • Deciding colour schemes for sites and documents
  • Choosing the text colour of buttons
  • Design that considers diversity in colour vision

Voorbeeld

Wanted to choose a text colour for a dark blue background. The difference from white was large, so white text read crisply.

Common stumbling blocks

  • Hard to see despite a large difference: depends on the combination. Check on an actual screen.
  • The background is an image: it may become hard to read depending on the image's brightness.

Frequently asked questions

Do the numbers alone decide readability?

They are a guide. Checking on an actual screen is important.

Samenvatting

Look at the brightness difference from white and black to decide the text colour. The larger the difference, the more readable.
