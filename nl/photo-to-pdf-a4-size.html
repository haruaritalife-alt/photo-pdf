Een PDF van foto's op A4-formaat maken

Gepubliceerd: 19 okt 2026 | Bijgewerkt: 19 okt 2026 | Over ons (in het Japans)

Belangrijkste punten
  • Is A4 vereist, zet het paginaformaat dan op “A4”
  • Foto's worden automatisch geplaatst om binnen A4 te passen
  • Kies marges door leesbaarheid en tekstgrootte af te wegen

When you turn photos into a PDF for submission, you often want them tidy on A4. With Photos to PDF you can choose the page size, either "A4" or "Same as photo", and the margin, either "None" or "Small".

A4 versus "Same as photo"

  • A4: every photo is placed so it fits inside an A4 page. The photo's aspect ratio does not change, and the leftover space becomes white margin. It suits documents you print and hand in, or anything that should look uniform.
  • Same as photo: each page is exactly the size of its photo. It suits material that is only viewed on screen, or when you want the whole picture to show with no margin.

Stappen

  1. Choose several photos at once with "Choose photos".
  2. Set the page size to "A4".
  3. Choose a margin, "Small" or "None".
  4. Pick the quality, create the PDF and save it.
A "Small" margin makes it less likely that the edges are cut off when you print.

When the orientations differ

Even if portrait and landscape shots are mixed, each one is scaled to fit inside the A4 page. A tall photo does look small on a landscape page, though. If readability matters, rotate the photos to the same orientation first so more of the page is used.

Common uses

  • Photographing copies of documents and putting them into one PDF
  • Photographing receipts for an expense report
  • Turning handwritten notes into a PDF on a readable paper size

Common stumbling blocks

  • The photo looks small: fitting it into A4 can shrink it. Set the margin to "None" to use more of the page.
  • The file is heavy: choose the "Small file" quality, or use the size limit.
  • Margin colour: margins are white. The background colour cannot be changed.

Frequently asked questions

Does the aspect ratio change?

No. Photos are not stretched; they keep their ratio.

Is there a limit on the number of photos?

There is no fixed limit, but very many photos can be heavy depending on your device's memory. Splitting them into groups of a few dozen is more stable.

Samenvatting

For printing and submitting, use A4 with a "Small" margin. When you care most about how the photo itself looks, choose "Same as photo".
