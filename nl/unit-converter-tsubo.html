Tsubo omrekenen naar vierkante meters en tatami

Gepubliceerd: 19 okt 2026 | Bijgewerkt: 19 okt 2026 | Over ons (in het Japans)

Belangrijkste punten
  • Kies Oppervlakte

  • Lees m² en tatami af

Property ads mix square metres, tsubo and tatami. "Area" in Unit Converter (Length, Weight, Area, Temperature and More) converts them.

Stappen

  1. For "Type", choose "Area".
  2. In "Number", enter the size.
  3. Choose the "From unit" and "To unit".
  4. Check the "Result".
A tatami mat uses the real-estate standard (1 mat = 1.62 m²).

Basic relations

  • 1 tsubo ≈ 3.3058 square metres
  • 1 tatami (real-estate notation) = 1.62 square metres
  • 1 tsubo ≈ 2 tatami

Conversion examples

  • 30 tsubo ≈ 99.17 square metres
  • 20 square metres ≈ 12.35 tatami
  • 6 tatami = 9.72 square metres ≈ 2.94 tsubo

Mind the size of a tatami

The size of a tatami differs by region (Edoma, Kyōma, Chūkyōma and so on). Real-estate ads follow a rule of showing 1 mat = 1.62 square metres. Actual mats may be larger or smaller.

Guide to home sizes

  • Studio: 6–8 tatami (about 10–13 square metres)
  • 1LDK: a living-dining-kitchen of 10–15 tatami
  • Family: 70–90 square metres (about 21–27 tsubo)

Voorbeeld

An ad said "8 jō". In square metres that is about 12.96. It became easier to compare with another room of the same size.

Common stumbling blocks

  • Does not match an actual tatami: tatami sizes differ by region.
  • "Jō" and "tatami": used with the same meaning.
  • Wall-centre versus inner dimensions: numbers differ with how area is measured.
