Wat te doen als je geen watermerk kunt toevoegen

Gepubliceerd: 19 okt 2026 | Bijgewerkt: 19 okt 2026 | Over ons (in het Japans)

Belangrijkste punten
  • Controleer of er tekst is ingevoerd
  • Controleer het formaat
  • Probeer een andere browser

You want to add a watermark but it does not go in, the text is invisible, or the image breaks. These are the problems that commonly occur with Add a Text Watermark to Images and how to deal with them.

1. The image cannot be processed

If "The image could not be processed. The format may not be supported." is shown:

  • For HEIC, first convert to JPG with "HEIC to JPG".
  • Check whether another app can open it.
  • Change to JPG or PNG and try again.

2. The watermark is invisible

  • Set the "Opacity" to "strong".
  • Set the "Colour" to one distinct from the background.
  • Increase the "Text size".
  • Check that the "Watermark text" is not empty.

3. The watermark is intrusive

  • Set the "Opacity" to "light".
  • Reduce the "Text size".
  • Set the "Position" to "Bottom right".

4. The text overflows or is cut off

  • Shorten the text.
  • Reduce the "Text size".

5. You want to remove the watermark from a saved image

After saving, it cannot be removed. Start again from the original image.

6. Some of several images failed

Images of a different format may be mixed in. Check one by one.

7. The ZIP will not open

Use an app that can unzip, or unzip on a computer.

8. The quality dropped

JPG loses a little quality on saving. Saving as PNG avoids degradation.

Order of checking

  1. Check the image format.
  2. Check the watermark text, position, opacity and colour.
  3. Check the "Preview".
  4. Open the saved image and check.
The original image does not change. You can redo it as many times as you like.

Common stumbling blocks

  • Some characters do not appear: check for unsupported characters.
  • Slow on a phone: a large image takes time. Try one at a time.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a limit on the length of the text?

If too long, it may not fit in the image. Shorten it.

Samenvatting

Adjust opacity, colour and size and check in the preview. Always keep the original image.
