Trouver le jour de la semaine d'une date

Publié le 19 oct. 2026 | Mis à jour le 19 oct. 2026 | À propos (en japonais)

Points clés
  • Saisissez une date
  • Voyez le jour de la semaine
  • Le vérifier

Once you know the date from a serial value, you can find the weekday. Here is how to get the date with Excel Serial Number and Date Converter and work out the weekday.

Étapes

  1. Enter the serial value in "Enter a value or date".
  2. Check the weekday of the date shown in "Date" on a calendar.

Calculating the weekday from a serial

The remainder after dividing by 7 decides the weekday (1900 system).

  • Remainder 1: Sunday
  • Remainder 2: Monday
  • Remainder 3: Tuesday
  • Remainder 4: Wednesday
  • Remainder 5: Thursday
  • Remainder 6: Friday
  • Remainder 0: Saturday
Before March 1, 1900, this may be off because of the 60 issue mentioned earlier.

Finding it with Excel functions

  • WEEKDAY(serial): returns 1 (Sun) to 7 (Sat)
  • TEXT(serial, "ddd"): returns a name such as "Mon"

Convient pour

  • Knowing the weekday of a numeric date
  • Calculating excluding weekends
  • Sorting by weekday

Exemple

Wanted the weekday of serial 45292. 45292 divided by 7 leaves 2: Monday, matching January 1, 2024 being a Monday.

Common stumbling blocks

  • Remainder mapping: differs by system (1900/1904).
  • WEEKDAY types: the argument switches Sunday-start or Monday-start.

Frequently asked questions

En résumé

Knowing the date gives the weekday. Even from the raw number, the remainder of 7 works it out.
