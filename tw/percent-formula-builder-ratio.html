計算佔比

釋出日期：2026 年 10 月 19 日｜最後更新：2026 年 10 月 19 日｜關於（日文）

要點
  • 選擇 RATIO
  • 輸入兩個數值
  • 複製

What percent of the whole is it? That's the ratio (composition share). "RATIO" in Build a Percentage Formula builds it.

步驟

  1. Choose "RATIO" in "Type".
  2. Enter the part's value as "First" and the whole as "Second".
  3. Copy the formula and paste.
It's first ÷ second: the part first, the whole second.

示例

With a whole of 500 and a part of 120:

  • =120/500 → 0.24 (24%)

With cells:

  • =A2/B2

The formatted formula

The second formula turns it into percent text with TEXT.

  • =TEXT(A2/B2,"0.0%")

It shows "24.0%". The result is text and can't be used in calculations.

Listing composition shares

Fix the grand total and compute each row's share.

  • Example: =B2/SUM($B$2:$B$10)

Copied down it makes a share column. The shares total 100%.

Formatting via cell format

Keep the formula as division and set the cell's display format to "Percent". It stays numeric and usable in calculations.

適合

  • Sales composition
  • Achievement rate (actual ÷ target)
  • Share of survey answers

Achievement rate

  • Example: actual ÷ target → =B2/C2

Over 100% means the target was met.

示例

Wanted each product's share of sales. Copying a fixed-total formula down made a share column.

Common stumbling blocks

  • The whole is 0: divides by 0 and errors.
  • Forgetting to fix the whole: the total range shifts on copying down. Add $.

Frequently asked questions

I want to change the decimal places

Change TEXT's "0.0%" to "0%" or "0.00%".

小結

Ratio is part ÷ whole. Make it a percent via cell format.
