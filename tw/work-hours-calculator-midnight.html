計算跨午夜的班次

釋出日期：2026 年 10 月 19 日｜最後更新：2026 年 10 月 19 日｜關於（日文）

要點
  • 輸入開始時間
  • 輸入結束時間
  • 檢視結果

Night shifts and late-night part-time jobs cross into the next day, for example from 22:00 to 6:00 the next morning. Work Hours Calculator (with break and pay) treats an end time earlier than the start time as a time on the next day.

步驟

  1. Enter the starting time (e.g. 22:00) in "Start time".
  2. Enter the next morning's finishing time (e.g. 06:00) in "End time".
  3. Enter the "Break (min)".
  4. Check "Working time" in the result.
If the end time is earlier (smaller) than the start time, it is automatically treated as a time on the next day.

示例

Start 22:00, end 6:00, break 60 minutes.

  • 22:00 to 6:00 the next day: 8 hours
  • Subtract the break: 7 hours
  • Working time: 7 hours 0 minutes

Range it can calculate

Work crossing days can be calculated if under 24 hours. Work of 24 hours or more (two days or more) is outside this tool.

  • Same start and end time: treated as 0 hours

Notes on crossing days

  • Date boundary: even if the start and end dates differ, it calculates by times only.
  • Daily totals: to split hours into daily totals, calculate in two parts split at 0:00.
  • Late-night pay: hours from 22:00 to 5:00 can be subject to a pay premium.

Calculating split by date

For example, to split a 22:00–6:00 shift by day:

  • Day 1: 22:00–24:00 (2 hours)
  • Day 2: 0:00–6:00 (6 hours)

Calculate each separately.

適合

  • Calculating hours for night shifts and duty
  • Checking hours of late-night part-time work
  • Checking hours of work crossing days

示例

A convenience store night shift from 22:00 to 7:00 the next morning with a 60-minute break. Working time was 8 hours 0 minutes. I could check an estimate of pay from the wage too.

Common stumbling blocks

  • Entering 24:00: enter as 0:00.
  • Entering the same time: results in 0 hours, not a 24-hour shift.

Frequently asked questions

Can work of 24 hours or more be calculated?

No. Split by day and calculate each.

小結

If the end is earlier than the start, it counts as next day. Night shift hours are calculated instantly too.
