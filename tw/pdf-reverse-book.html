如何糾正掃描的右開本書的頁面順序

釋出日期：2026 年 10 月 19 日｜最後更新：2026 年 10 月 19 日｜關於（日文）

要點
  • 選擇 PDF
  • 倒序
  • 需要的話拆分跨頁

You scanned a book or booklet one page at a time and the order came out wrong. In a right-bound book (a vertical-text book, manga and so on), the order of left and right pages is the reverse of a left-bound book. Here is how to fix the order with Reverse the Page Order of a PDF.

What a right-bound book is

A Japanese vertical-text book is bound on the right and its pages go from right to left.

  • Left-bound (horizontal-text books): read the left page, then the right page.
  • Right-bound (vertical-text books, manga): read the right page, then the left page.

When scanning a spread

If you scan a two-page spread as one image, left and right are in a single sheet. This tool cannot swap left and right inside a single sheet.

When scanning one page at a time

If you scan a right-bound book from the left in order, the page order can be the reverse of the reading order.

  • The whole thing is backwards: reverse.
  • Left and right pages of a spread are swapped: swap two at a time.

步驟

  1. 用「選擇 PDF」選擇 PDF。
  2. Choose the "Arrangement" for your situation.
  • The whole thing runs from last to first: "Reverse the pages (last to first)".
  • Left and right pages are swapped: "Swap two pages at a time (1↔2, 3↔4…)".
  1. Press "Reorder and create PDF".
  2. Check the order in the "Preview".
  3. Save with "Save PDF".
If the cover or first page stands alone, the pairing for swapping can be off.

When the cover stands alone

If a spread follows a single-page cover, the pairing is shifted by one. You need a workaround such as making a PDF without the cover and then swapping.

  1. Extract or delete the cover.
  2. Swap the rest two at a time.
  3. Put the cover back at the front with the merge tool.

示例

In a manga scan the left and right of the spreads were reversed. Exclude the cover, swap two at a time and restore the cover with the merge tool.

Handling copyrighted works

Keep scanning books to personal use. You may not distribute or publish them.

Common stumbling blocks

  • The cover throws it off: process without the cover.
  • A spread is a single image: this tool cannot split it. Split left and right with the "Split PDF spreads into single pages" tool.
  • The orientation is wrong: fix it with the rotate tool.

Frequently asked questions

How can I tell right-bound from left-bound?

A vertical-text book read from the right is right-bound.

What happens to page numbers?

After reordering, you can add them again with the page number tool.

小結

For right-bound books, watch the left-right order. Reverse the whole thing or swap two at a time, and check in the preview.
