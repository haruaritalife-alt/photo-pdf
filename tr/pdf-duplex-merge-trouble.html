Ön ve Arka Yüzleri Birleştiremiyorsanız Ne Yapmalı

Yayınlanma: 19 Eki 2026 | Güncellenme: 19 Eki 2026 | Hakkında (Japonca)

Önemli noktalar
  • İkisinin de seçildiğinden emin olun
  • Parola olup olmadığını kontrol edin
  • Başka bir tarayıcı deneyin

You want to combine fronts and backs but cannot, the order is wrong, or pages drift. These are the problems that commonly occur with Merge Front and Back Scans into One PDF and how to deal with them.

1. The order is wrong

  • Try the "Order of the back-side PDF" the other way round.
  • If you flipped the stack, "Reversed"; if you re-fed sheet by sheet, "As is".

2. The page counts do not match

  • The number of fronts and backs differs. Suspect a missed scan or a double feed.
  • If you left out sheets blank on the back, add blank pages to match the count.

3. The back side is upside down

Rotate the back PDF first, then combine. Explained in a separate guide.

4. It drifts from partway

A sheet is missing somewhere. Find the page where the drift begins and check the original scan.

5. Blanks are mixed in

After combining, remove them with the tool that automatically finds and deletes blank pages.

6. A PDF cannot be chosen or opened

  • Remove the password from a protected PDF first.
  • Check it is not a broken PDF, and whether another app can open it.

7. Processing is slow

Many pages take time. Wait a while.

8. You notice a mistake after combining

A saved PDF cannot be fixed. Make it again from the original two PDFs.

Order of checking

  1. Did you choose "① front" and "② back" the wrong way round?
  2. Check the "Order of the back-side PDF".
  3. Check the page counts of both.
  4. Check the first few pages in the "Preview".
Keep the original two PDFs even after combining. You can redo it.

Common stumbling blocks

  • You chose fronts and backs the wrong way round: page 1 becomes a back. Choose again.
  • The preview is too small to see: zoom in and see whether the content connects.

Frequently asked questions

Can I combine three or more PDFs?

This tool combines two: fronts and backs. For several documents, combine one at a time, then join them with the merge tool.

Özet

Check the front/back choice, the order and the page counts. Look at the first few pages in the preview before saving.
