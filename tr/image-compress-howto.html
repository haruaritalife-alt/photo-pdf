Görüntü Dosya Boyutunu Küçültme (Sıkıştırma ve Yeniden Boyutlandırma)

Yayınlanma: 19 Eki 2026 | Güncellenme: 19 Eki 2026 | Hakkında (Japonca)

Önemli noktalar
  • Kalite ve görüntü boyutlarıyla (uzun kenar) küçültün
  • Birkaç görüntüyü aynı anda işleyin
  • Kullanmadan önce sonucu kontrol edin

A photo taken on a phone can be several MB on its own. It will not attach to an email, it will not fit a form, and a blog loads slowly. Compress & Resize Images makes images smaller and lighter in those cases. You choose the quality, the size (pixels on the longer side) and the format, and can process several images at once. Your files are never sent off your device.

Adımlar

  1. With "Choose images", select images. You can choose several at once (JPEG, PNG, WebP and so on).
  2. Check the list under "Selected images" and remove any you do not need with "Delete".
  3. Choose "Quality": "Small file", "Normal" or "High".
  4. Choose "Size (longer side)": "Original", "2000px", "1600px", "1200px" or "800px".
  5. Choose the "Format": JPEG or WebP.
  6. Press "Compress".
  7. Save each with "Save", or all together with "Save all (ZIP)".
Saved files get "_small" added to the name. The original images are not overwritten.

Two ways to make it lighter

  • Lower the quality: leaves out information that barely affects the look to shrink the file.
  • Reduce the size: cuts the number of pixels. It is very effective, and for viewing on screen you can hardly tell the difference.

Combining the two makes it much lighter.

A rough guide

  • Viewing on screen, sending by email: quality "Normal", size "1600px" or "1200px"
  • Blogs and websites: "Normal" and "1200px". To go lighter still, use WebP
  • Printing or viewing enlarged: "High" with "Original" or "2000px"

Reading the result

When it finishes, the original size and the compressed size are shown. If the result is larger than the original, it says so and tells you to use a lighter setting or keep the original.

Common stumbling blocks

  • A transparent background turned white: JPEG cannot keep transparency. To keep it, choose WebP, though in some browsers it is saved as PNG.
  • You chose WebP but got PNG: your browser cannot create WebP.
  • HEIC photos cannot be read: first convert them with the tool that turns HEIC into JPG.

Frequently asked questions

How many can I process at once?

There is no fixed limit. A large number is a load on your device, so a few dozen at a time is recommended.

Is the original image kept?

Yes. The result is saved as a new file.

Özet

Choose the quality, size and format and press "Compress". Check the result for readability and appearance before you use it.
