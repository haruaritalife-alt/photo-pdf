Tek Taraflı Besleyiciyle İki Yüzü Tarama

Yayınlanma: 19 Eki 2026 | Güncellenme: 19 Eki 2026 | Hakkında (Japonca)

Önemli noktalar
  • Tüm ön yüzleri tarayın
  • Desteyi çevirin ve arka yüzleri tarayın
  • Birleştirin

Even a feeder (ADF) scanner that does not support duplex can scan a double-sided document. Scan the fronts and backs separately and combine them with Merge Front and Back Scans into One PDF.

Scanning steps

  1. Square up the stack and load it in the scanner.
  2. Scan all the fronts only and save as a PDF (this is the "front-side PDF").
  3. Flip the finished stack over as it is.
  4. Scan all the backs only and save as a separate PDF (this is the "back-side PDF").

Combining steps

  1. With "① Choose the front-side PDF", choose the front PDF.
  2. With "② Choose the back-side PDF", choose the back PDF.
  3. Under "Order of the back-side PDF", choose "Reversed" (when you flipped the stack).
  4. Press "Combine into one PDF" and check the "Preview".
  5. Save with "Save PDF".
Do not reorder sheets midway. If the order of the stack is disturbed, they will not match.

Scanning tips

  • Square the paper before loading (misalignment tends to jam)
  • The stack comes out in the order the fronts were scanned. Flip it without changing its orientation.
  • When scanning the backs, watch the paper orientation (it easily ends up upside down)

If the orientation is reversed

Depending on the direction you flip, the back side may end up upside down. In that case rotate the back PDF before using it. Explained in a separate guide.

Örnek

A 20-sheet double-sided document. Make a 20-page front PDF and a 20-page back PDF and combine with "Reversed" into one 40-page PDF.

Common stumbling blocks

  • The order does not match: check that the order was not changed within the stack.
  • Page counts differ: possibly a missed scan or double feed.
  • Backs are the wrong way up: fix with the rotation tool.

Frequently asked questions

Is this unnecessary with a duplex scanner?

Yes. The duplex scan setting gives one PDF.

Özet

All the fronts → flip the stack → all the backs. Combine the two PDFs with this tool.
