Paano Higpitan ang Pag-print, Pagkopya at Pag-edit ng PDF

Nailathala: Okt 19, 2026 | Na-update: Okt 19, 2026 | Tungkol (Japanese)

Mahahalagang punto
  • Piliin ang PDF
  • Lagyan ng tsek ang mga aksyon
  • I-save

When handing over a PDF, you sometimes want it viewed only, not printed or copied. "Also forbid (optional)" in Add a Password to a PDF lets you set prohibitions on printing, copying text and editing.

Mga hakbang

  1. With "Choose a PDF", choose the PDF.
  2. Enter the "Password" and "Password (confirm)".
  3. Under "Also forbid (optional)", tick what you want to forbid.
  • Pag-print
  • Pagkopya ng text
  • Pag-edit
  1. Save with "Save PDF".
If you forget the password, this PDF can no longer be opened. Be sure to keep a record of it.

What can be forbidden

  • Printing: it can no longer be printed on paper.
  • Copying text: text can no longer be selected and copied.
  • Editing: the PDF's content and structure can no longer be changed.

Mabuti para sa

  • Making a quotation or proposal view-only
  • Discouraging unauthorised copying of material
  • Preventing alteration of material you distribute

Note (important)

These prohibitions are like a request to the viewing app. Not every app will honour them.

  • There may be apps or tools that ignore the restrictions.
  • Screenshots cannot be prevented.
  • Prohibitions help deter casual copying but are not strong protection.

For content you truly want to protect, set an open password and hand it only to people who know the password.

Alin ang gagamitin

  • Open password: limits who can see the contents.
  • Forbidding printing, copying, editing: limits what those who can open it may do.

You can also combine both.

Halimbawa

Handing a proposal to a business partner. Set an open password and forbid printing and copying. The other party can review the content on screen.

Common stumbling blocks

  • The recipient was troubled by being unable to print: consider whether it is truly needed and the recipient's convenience.
  • Restrictions get removed: it cannot be prevented with certainty.
  • You cannot edit it either: keep the original PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Can I remove the prohibitions later?

If you know the password, you can remove them with the tool that unlocks passwords.

Buod

Printing, copying and editing can be forbidden, but it is not strong protection. Protect important things with an open password.

Gamitin ang “Magdagdag ng Password sa PDF” (libre)

Mga tool na bagay dito

  • Alisin ang Password sa PDF: Alisin ang password at mga restriksyon sa PDF na alam mo ang password.
  • Itim-an (I-redact) ang mga Bahagi ng PDF: I-drag sa mga bahagi ng PDF na gusto mong itago at itim-an ang mga ito. Nagiging larawan ang mga na-redact na pahina, kaya nawawala ang text sa ilalim.
  • Tingnan at Alisin ang mga Property ng PDF (May-akda, Pamagat): Tingnan ang may-akda, pamagat, software at petsang nakaimbak sa PDF, saka alisin o i-edit ang mga ito.
