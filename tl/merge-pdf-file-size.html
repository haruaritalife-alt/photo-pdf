Ano ang Gagawin Kapag Masyadong Malaki ang Pinagsamang PDF

Nailathala: Okt 19, 2026 | Na-update: Okt 19, 2026 | Tungkol (Japanese)

Mahahalagang punto
  • Ang pagsasama lang ay nagpapanatili ng halos pinagsamang laki ng mga orihinal
  • Magandang magkasabay ang pag-alis ng mga pahina at pag-compress
  • Hindi na napipili ang text pagkatapos mag-compress

After merging PDFs, the file may be bigger than you expected, too large to email or to fit in a submission form. Here is how to think about it and what to do. Merge PDFs places the contents of the original PDFs as they are, so quality does not drop, but the size ends up close to the total of the originals.

Why it gets big

Merging only joins the pages; it does not make the content smaller. Merging a 10 MB PDF and a 15 MB PDF gives roughly 25 MB. PDFs with many photos or scanned images tend to be heavy.

Ang gagawin

  1. Make the originals lighter before merging: often only one heavy PDF is the cause.
  2. Compress after merging: use the "Compress PDF" tool to shrink it to the recipient's limit.
  3. Reduce pages: extract only the pages you need.
  4. Send in parts: if it still does not fit, split it in two.
Compression turns pages into images, so you can no longer select or search the text.

Inirerekomendang pagkakasunod-sunod

  1. Pagsamahin
  2. Check the page count and the contents
  3. I-compress kung kinakailangan

Typical size limits

  • Email attachments: many mail services cap around 25 MB, though the recipient's limit may be smaller.
  • Application forms: often a few MB up to 10 MB. Check the guidelines.

Common stumbling blocks

  • Text looks blurry after compressing: set the quality to "Normal" or higher and try again.
  • Too heavy even before compression: find out which original is heavy and lighten it first.
  • Extra pages are included: remove blank pages and unneeded documents.

Frequently asked questions

Can merging alone make it smaller?

No. Merging joins the content as it is.

Is there a guide for compression?

Try "Normal" first, and use the size limit if it still does not fit.

Buod

A merged file is close to the sum of the originals. If it is heavy, compress after merging or lighten the originals first.
