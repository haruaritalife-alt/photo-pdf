Ano ang Lorem ipsum at paano ito gamitin

Nailathala: Okt 19, 2026 | Na-update: Okt 19, 2026 | Tungkol (Japanese)

Mahahalagang punto
  • Piliin ang Lorem ipsum
  • Itakda ang bilang ng pangungusap
  • Kopyahin

In design samples, have you seen meaningless text like "Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet…"? That's "Lorem ipsum". Try making it with Dummy Text Generator (Japanese, Lorem ipsum).

What Lorem ipsum is

Latin-like placeholder text with no meaning. It has long been used in printing and design. It's based on old writing but altered so no meaning can be read.

Why it is used

With meaningful text, people read the content and can't focus on the design. With meaningless text, attention goes to appearance alone.

  • Checking text size and line spacing
  • Checking paragraph length
  • Checking overall balance

Mga hakbang

  1. Choose "Lorem ipsum (Latin-style)" under "Type".
  2. Enter "Sentences (1–50)".
  3. Pindutin ang “Gumawa”.
  4. Copy with "Copy".
A paragraph break occurs every 4 sentences, and sentences are joined with half-width spaces.

Paano gamitin

Paste into a design tool or HTML body. Once the layout is decided, replace with the real text.

Mabuti para sa

  • Designing English sites and documents
  • Making design templates
  • Making font samples
  • Checking display of English screens

Halimbawa

The body text under the heading of an English landing page wasn't decided yet. Entering 8 sentences and pasting two paragraphs let me check the layout.

Mga tala

  • It's placeholder text, so always replace it with real text before publishing.
  • For Japanese designs, Japanese dummy text looks closer.

Common stumbling blocks

  • Forgetting to replace: take care that Lorem ipsum doesn't remain after publication.
  • Search engines: leftover meaningless text may hurt evaluation.
