Paano Maglagay ng Blangkong Pahina sa PDF (Libre, Puwede sa Telepono)

Nailathala: Okt 19, 2026 | Na-update: Okt 19, 2026 | Tungkol (Japanese)

Mahahalagang punto
  • Pumili ng PDF
  • Ilagay kung saan ipapasok
  • I-save ang PDF

You want a blank page at a chapter break. You want to add a blank for double-sided printing. You want empty pages for notes. Insert Blank Pages into a PDF is a tool that inserts blank pages into a PDF. The PDF is never sent off your device.

Three ways to insert

Choose from "How to insert" under "Choose where to insert blanks".

  • Insert after specified pages: specify page numbers and insert a blank after each.
  • Insert after every page: inserts one blank after every page.
  • If the page count is odd, add one at the end (for double-sided printing): for an odd page count, adds a blank at the end to make it even.

Mga hakbang

  1. With "Choose a PDF", choose one PDF.
  2. Choose "How to insert".
  3. For "Insert after specified pages", enter numbers in "Page numbers (e.g. 2,5. 0 means the start)".
  4. Press "Insert blanks and create PDF".
  5. Check the "Preview" and save with "Save PDF".
The size of the blank page is the same as the previous page.

How to enter page numbers

  • "2": inserts after page 2.
  • "2,5": inserts after pages 2 and 5.
  • "0": inserts at the start (before page 1).
  • Specify with digits and commas.

Kailan gagamitin

  • Chapter breaks: a blank after the last page of each chapter.
  • A blank before the cover: "0" for the start.
  • Double-sided printing: add a blank at the end of an odd-page PDF to make it even.
  • For notes: insert a blank after every page to make room for writing.

Halimbawa

In a 10-page PDF, insert blanks after pages 3 and 7. Enter "3,7". The result is 12 pages.

Common stumbling blocks

  • "Specify page numbers with digits and commas" appears: full-width or other characters were entered. Use half-width digits and commas.
  • "There was no blank to insert" appears: this appears if you choose "add at the end if odd" when the count is not odd.
  • A blank is not where you expected: check the page number specification.

Frequently asked questions

Can I change the blank page size?

It becomes the same size as the previous page.

Does the original PDF change?

No. A new PDF is made.

Buod

Choose how to insert, specify page numbers and insert the blanks. Check in the preview, then save.
