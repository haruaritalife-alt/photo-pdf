Cosa succede se fronti e retro hanno un numero diverso di pagine?

Pubblicato: 19 ott 2026 | Aggiornato: 19 ott 2026 | Informazioni (in giapponese)

Punti chiave
  • Una differenza di uno è normale
  • Le pagine in più vanno in fondo
  • Valuta di rifare la scansione

The front PDF and back PDF may have different page counts. Merge Front and Back Scans into One PDF can combine them in this case too, but take care over the resulting sequence.

What happens

Fronts and backs are interleaved, and when one runs out first, the leftover pages go at the end. The tool shows "the page counts of front and back differ, so the leftover pages are at the end".

Esempio

6 front pages and 5 back pages. → front 1, back 1, front 2, back 2… front 5, back 5, then front 6.

Passaggi

  1. Choose the PDFs with "① Choose the front-side PDF" and "② Choose the back-side PDF".
  2. Choose the "Order of the back-side PDF".
  3. Press "Combine into one PDF" and check the "Preview".
  4. If "* The page counts do not match." is shown, check the cause.
  5. Save with "Save PDF".
If you save with mismatched counts, fronts and backs may be misaligned from partway.

Main causes of differing counts

  • A sheet jammed or fed double during scanning
  • Sheets blank on the back were left out of the back scan
  • A miscount of sheets

Cosa fare

  1. Count the sheets for fronts and backs.
  2. Rescan or make up the shortfall.
  3. For sheets blank on the back, add blank pages to match the count.

Adding blanks to match

If some sheets have text only on the front and you left them out of the back scan, add blank pages to the back PDF to match the count. Then combine with this tool.

Common stumbling blocks

  • The order drifts partway: a sheet is missing somewhere. Find where the drift begins.
  • A leftover at the end: if there are more fronts, the back of the last sheet may not have been scanned.

Frequently asked questions

Can I still make it with just a warning if counts differ?

Yes. It can be made, but checking is needed.

Riepilogo
