Dividere il conto senza caricare nulla

Pubblicato: 19 ott 2026 | Aggiornato: 19 ott 2026 | Informazioni (in giapponese)

Punti chiave
  • Inserisci un valore
  • Guarda il risultato
  • Copialo

Fee amounts and head counts relate to money among friends or at work. You may hesitate to put them out. Split the Bill Calculator (with rounding) calculates inside the browser without sending the amounts and headcount you enter out.

Come funziona

  • The amounts and headcount you enter are processed only inside your browser.
  • Division and rounding-up calculations also happen on your device.
  • Neither input nor results are sent to a server or stored.

Passaggi

  1. Enter "Total (yen)" and "Number of people".
  2. Choose the "Rounding-up unit".
  3. Controlla il risultato.
What you enter is processed inside this device. It is sent nowhere.

How to confirm

  • It works even with the connection cut (airplane mode).
  • Browser developer tools can confirm that there is no network traffic.

Still be careful about

  • Shared computers: when finished, close the page or clear the input.
  • Sharing results: when sharing via chat apps etc., adding the basis (total) helps people accept the amount.
  • Organisation money: settle company or group money following the organisation's accounting rules.

Uses it suits

  • Settling with friends and family
  • Calculating fees for workplace parties
  • Calculating costs for clubs and local gatherings

Esempio

Wanted to calculate the fee for a company drinking party. Since the numbers relate to the company budget, didn't want to enter them into an outside service, but being calculated in the browser, I could use it with peace of mind.

Common stumbling blocks

  • Input vanished on closing the page: by design nothing is saved. Note the result.
  • Calculation mistakes: to be safe, check that the total collected matches "Total collected".

Frequently asked questions

Can the operator learn the amounts?

No. They aren't sent outside, so they can't.

Riepilogo

The amounts and headcount aren't sent outside. Just take care in sharing results and managing money.
