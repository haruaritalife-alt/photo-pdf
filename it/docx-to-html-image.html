Immagini e colori sono inclusi?

Pubblicato: 19 ott 2026 | Aggiornato: 19 ott 2026 | Informazioni (in giapponese)

Punti chiave
  • Scopri

Word manuscripts contain photos and figures, and use colours and typefaces. Even when converted with Convert Word to HTML, images, text colours and fonts are not included in the HTML. Know in advance what comes in and what doesn't.

What is included

  • Headings
  • Bold and italics
  • Bullet and numbered lists
  • Tables

What isn't included

  • Images, photos and figures
  • Text colours
  • Fonts (typefaces)
  • Page margins and columns
  • Headers and footers
Images can be extracted separately with the "Extract images from Word" tool. When you want them in the HTML, add them afterwards.

Flow when you want to use images

  1. Turn the text into HTML with this tool.
  2. Extract Word's images as a ZIP with the image-extraction tool.
  3. Upload the images to the site.
  4. In the HTML, add image tags where you want the images.

Why they aren't included

Images and colours vary with the site's design and where images are stored. Converting only the structure of the text makes it easy to paste into any site.

When you want colours and text sizes

Setting them in the site's design (CSS) is the usual way. Bringing Word's colours in as they are may clash with the site's look.

Adatto a

  • Articles centred on text
  • A workflow of adding images in the blog's screen

Esempio

Wrote an article with images in Word. Turned the text into HTML with this tool, extracted the images, uploaded them to the blog and inserted them in the right positions.

Common stumbling blocks

  • You lose track of image positions: write "Image 1 here" in Word.
  • Colours aren't applied: set them on the site side.

Frequently asked questions

Can images be converted together?

Not with this tool. Extract them separately and add them.

Riepilogo

Only the text's structure is converted. Tidy images and colours on the site side.
