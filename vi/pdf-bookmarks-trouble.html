Việc cần làm khi không hoạt động

Đăng: 19/10/2026 | Cập nhật: 19/10/2026 | Giới thiệu (tiếng Nhật)

Điểm chính
  • Kiểm tra tệp
  • Kiểm tra dung lượng
  • Thử thiết bị khác

Bookmarks were added but don't show in the viewer. Jumps go to the wrong page. Here are troubles that commonly occur with View and Add PDF Bookmarks and how to handle them.

Common troubles

  • Bookmarks not shown: check that the viewer's bookmark (contents) pane is open.
  • Jumps to the wrong page: PDF page numbers may differ from printed ones.
  • "No bookmarks entered" appears: the input field is empty. Enter "page number title".
  • "(No bookmarks)" appears: the original PDF has none. This is normal.
  • Original bookmarks vanished: by design, adding replaces them.

Steps (when it doesn't go well)

  1. Look at the input and check it is one per line in the form "page number title".
  2. Check page numbers are half-width.
  3. Check page numbers are no greater than the PDF's total pages.
  4. Check in another viewer (such as a browser).
In some viewers the bookmark pane is closed by default. Try opening the left panel.

Input examples

  • Correct: "5 Chapter 1 Introduction"
  • Incorrect: "Chapter 1 Introduction" (no page number)
  • Incorrect: "５ Chapter 1" (full-width digit)

Page number shifts

If the cover and contents aren't counted as pages, printed "page 1" and PDF "page 3" differ. Enter numbers while watching the viewer's page number display.

For password-protected PDFs

PDFs protected by a password may not be processed. Try with a PDF whose protection is removed.

Uses it suits

  • Pre-checks before trouble
  • Checking the input format

Ví dụ

A bookmark jumped to the wrong page. On checking, I had entered printed numbers. After re-attaching with PDF page numbers, it jumped correctly.

Common stumbling blocks

  • Large files: processing can take time.
  • Scanned PDFs: bookmarks can be added to image-only PDFs too.

Frequently asked questions

Does it look different by viewer?

It can. Checking in several viewers gives peace of mind.

Tóm tắt

Review format, half-width digits and page numbers. That solves most troubles.

Dùng “Xem và thêm dấu trang PDF” (miễn phí)

Công cụ hay dùng cùng

  • Gộp PDF: Gộp nhiều tệp PDF thành một theo thứ tự bạn chọn.
  • Thêm số trang vào PDF: Thêm số trang vào PDF, chọn vị trí và kiểu.
  • Xem và xóa thuộc tính PDF (tác giả, tiêu đề): Xem tác giả, tiêu đề, phần mềm và ngày tháng lưu trong PDF, rồi xóa hoặc chỉnh sửa.
