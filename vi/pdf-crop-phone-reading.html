Làm PDF dễ đọc hơn trên điện thoại: cắt lề để phóng to chữ

Đăng: 19/10/2026 | Cập nhật: 19/10/2026 | Giới thiệu (tiếng Nhật)

Điểm chính
  • Tự động cắt lề
  • Khớp vùng trên tất cả các trang
  • Mở trên điện thoại và kiểm tra độ dễ đọc

Open a PDF on a phone and, even though it fills the screen, the margins are wide and the text small. Even enlarged, you have to scroll sideways, which is hard to read. Trimming the margins with Crop PDF Margins makes the text display larger on the same screen.

Why it becomes easier to read

A phone fits the whole page to the screen width. The wider the margin, the smaller the content. Trimming the margins lets the content fill the screen.

Các bước

  1. With "Choose a PDF", choose the PDF you want to read.
  2. Set "How to crop" to "Trim margins automatically (per page)".
  3. Set "Margin to leave around" to "A little (3 mm)".
  4. Press "Crop" and check the preview.
  5. Save and open it on your phone.
Because the crop range differs per page, the page sizes will vary. While reading on, the degree of enlargement may change.

When you want the page sizes consistent

Choosing "Use the same area for all pages" crops all pages to the same area. The look is stable and easier to read through.

Two-column pages and figures

On pages with many figures, automatic trimming may cut a figure. Check in the preview and, if there is a problem, increase "Margin to leave around" or use "Specify four sides in mm".

Ví dụ

A paper PDF: wide margins and small text. After trimming the margins, the text displays larger on the same phone screen.

Other tricks

  • Turn the phone sideways
  • Rotate the pages and read in landscape
  • To enlarge text, use the PDF's zoom

Common stumbling blocks

  • The edge of the text is cut: set the margin to "More (8 mm)".
  • Page sizes vary: use "Use the same area for all pages".
  • The effect is small: for PDFs with small original margins, the change is small.

Frequently asked questions

Does cropping lower image quality?

No. The page content is unchanged.

Does the file get smaller?

Cropping alone changes it very little.

Tóm tắt

Trim margins automatically and leave "A little (3 mm)". If you want consistent page sizes, use the same-area setting.
